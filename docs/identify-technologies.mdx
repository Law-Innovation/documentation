Depending on which technologies you use and how you configure them, they will pose varying degrees of data protection risks to your website visitors. The fewer risks your technologies pose, the more your users will trust your service and the more likely they will be to consent to the processing of their data.

To build as much trust as possible with your users, in the [Configure your tools](/tools-configuration) section we'll show you which technologies are most effective at helping you minimise risks, and how you can configure them accordingly.

## How to identify your website technologies

If you have already set up a website and are unsure which technologies process your visitors' personal data, there are various methods you can use to identify the technologies running on your website:

1. **Website scanners:** Automated tools can help you identify cookies, scripts, trackers, third-party connections, and other technologies. Several free or open-source tools are available, including:
   - **[EDPB Website Auditing Tool](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/documents/support-pool-of-experts/edpb-website-auditing-tool_en):** A free, open-source tool developed under a European Data Protection Board project to support website privacy audits. It provides detailed insights into cookies, tracking technologies, and third-party connections.
   - **[Webbkoll](https://webbkoll.5july.net/) and [Blacklight](https://themarkup.org/blacklight):** Free, browser-based tools that offer a quick and easy first check of cookies, trackers, and other privacy-related website characteristics.
   - **[OpenWPM](https://github.com/openwpm/OpenWPM):** An advanced open-source framework for developers and researchers that enables more detailed, automated analysis of website technologies and tracking behaviour.
2. **Your AI assistant:** You can ask your preferred AI tool to help identify website technologies, analyse scanner results, and explain how the detected technologies may process personal data. You can also connect your AI to Consenter Manager so it can help you configure your banner (see [Connect your AI](/ai-agents)).
3. **Manual checks:** You can review your website's source code, browser developer tools, tag management system, and the documentation provided by your technology providers.

## How reliable are these methods?

Website scanners differ in their detection methods, scope, and accuracy. Some technologies may only become active after specific user interactions, on certain pages, or under particular conditions. As a result, even deterministic scanners may not detect every relevant technology.

AI tools can also help you identify technologies, analyse scanner results and understand their potential data processing purposes. However, AI models operate non-deterministically and may produce different or incorrect results when analysing the same information.

**We therefore recommend combining AI-based analysis with a deterministic website scanner wherever possible.** Your AI tool can also help you interpret the scanner's findings and use them to configure your Consenter Banner (see [Connect your AI](/ai-agents)).

The scanners listed above are provided as examples only. Consenter does not assess or endorse the reliability of individual scanners.

## Before you proceed

Make sure you understand which technologies are running on your website, what personal data they process, and for which purposes they are used.

Once you have reviewed your results, proceed to [Select Your Purposes](/purposes) to determine which of the identified processing operations require your visitors' consent.
