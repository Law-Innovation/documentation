Typical processing purposes: Support marketing analytics, Receive personalised marketing offers

> This guide explains how to implement different privacy configurations for Marketo Engage (Munchkin tracking) and how to reflect them in the Consenter Manager.
>
> 1. Configure the third-party service.
> 2. Map the selected configuration in the Consenter Manager.
> 3. Update your privacy policy to reflect the actual implementation.
>
> The configurations are comparative examples, not guarantees of legal compliance. Verify that the guide matches your product edition, implementation, and current provider documentation.

## At a glance

**Scope.** The Munchkin JavaScript tracking code and Marketo Forms 2.0. Adobe Inc. is a US company. Sources were checked on 16 November 2026.

**What the service does in this scope.** Munchkin sets the `_mkto_trk` cookie, specific to each second-level domain, with a default lifespan of 2 years (730 days) [S1]. On a visitor's first visit, an anonymous lead record is created whose primary key is the Munchkin cookie [S2]. `Munchkin.init()` accepts configuration settings: `cookieAnon` controls whether a cookie is created for all leads upon visiting, and `cookieLifeDays` sets the expiry of newly created cookies [S3]. Visitors can opt out with `marketo_opt_out=true`, which sets `mkto_opt_out` [S1]. Forms 2.0 can be embedded without Munchkin, and thus without a new cookie [S4].

**Configurations included.**

| Configuration | Summary |
|---|---|
| Configuration A — Lower risk | Forms 2.0 only, no Munchkin; form data processed on submission. |
| Configuration B — Medium risk | Munchkin after consent; `cookieAnon: false`; 180-day cookie. |
| Configuration C — Higher risk (default behaviour) | Munchkin after consent; anonymous and known leads tracked; 2-year cookie; email link identification. |

**Configuration without prior consent (A).** Without Munchkin no tracking cookie is set [S4]; form data is processed on the legal basis of the form's purpose (Art. 6 GDPR). Whether the Forms library itself accesses device storage should be verified in the browser.

## Step 1 — Configure Marketo Engage

| # | Configuration area | Where to configure it | Configuration A — Lower risk | Configuration B — Medium risk | Configuration C — Higher risk (default behaviour) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Processing purposes | Operator decision | — (form only) | Support marketing analytics | Support marketing analytics; Receive personalised marketing offers |
| 2 | Munchkin | Website code | Not loaded | `Munchkin.init()` after consent | `Munchkin.init()` after consent |
| 3 | `cookieAnon` | `Munchkin.init` config [S3] | — | `false` | `true` (default) |
| 4 | `cookieLifeDays` | `Munchkin.init` config [S3] | — | 180 | 730 (default) |
| 5 | Email link tracking (`mkt_tok`) | Email settings | — | Off | On |

## Step 2 — Map the configuration in the Consenter Manager

**Processing location.** Adobe is a US enterprise subject to 18 U.S.C. § 2713 [S5]; list the United States in all configurations with an entry.

| Consenter Manager field | Configuration A — Lower risk | Configuration B — Medium risk | Configuration C — Higher risk (default behaviour) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Entry needed | Optional (form processing only) | Yes | Yes |
| Processing purposes | — | Support marketing analytics | Support marketing analytics, Receive personalised marketing offers |
| Tracking method | No tracking | First party tracking (cross-session) | First party tracking (cross-session) |
| Identifier | Direct identifier (on submission) | Device identifiers, Direct identifier | Device identifiers, Direct identifier |
| Data categories | User-provided data, direct identifier | Browsing and interaction data, Device identifiers, IP address, User-provided data, direct identifier | As B, plus Users' profiles |
| Legal role of data recipient | Processor | Processor | Processor |
| Personalisation model | No personalisation | Group based (behaviour) | Profile based |
| Maximum storage duration | Per lead retention | 180 days (cookie) | 2 years (cookie) |
| Processing location | Contract region; United States | Contract region; United States | Contract region; United States |
