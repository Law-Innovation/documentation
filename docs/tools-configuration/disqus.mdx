<Callout type="info" title="Overview">
This guide explains how to implement different privacy configurations for the Disqus comment widget and how to reflect them in the Consenter Manager.

1. Configure the third-party service.
2. Map the selected configuration in the Consenter Manager.
3. Update your privacy policy to reflect the actual implementation.

The configurations are comparative examples, not guarantees of legal compliance. Verify that the guide matches your product edition, implementation, and current provider documentation.
</Callout>

## At a glance

**Scope.** The Disqus comment embed (Disqus Inc. / Big Heads Labs Inc., San Francisco, US [S1][S2]). Sources were checked on 16 November 2026.

**What the service does in this scope.** The Norwegian DPA found that Disqus collected data through cookies placed on visitors' devices and passed it to third-party advertising partners and its parent company, and announced a fine [S3]. Publishers can disable data tracking for their forum via the **Tracking** setting under **Settings > Advanced**; users can opt out on the Data Sharing Settings page [S4]. Ads within and below comments can be switched off under **Settings > Ads**; removing ads entirely requires a paid plan [S5].

**Configurations included.**

| Configuration | Summary |
|---|---|
| Configuration A — Medium risk | Tracking disabled in **Settings > Advanced**; ads off (paid plan); widget after Consenter consent. |
| Configuration B — Higher risk (default configuration) | Default tracking and ads; widget after consent. |

## Step 1 — Configure Disqus

| # | Configuration area | Where to configure it | Configuration A — Medium risk | Configuration B — Higher risk (default configuration) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Processing purposes | Operator decision | Unlock additional website features | Unlock additional website features; Customise online ads |
| 2 | Tracking | **Settings > Advanced > Tracking** [S4] | Disabled | Enabled |
| 3 | Ads | **Settings > Ads** [S5] | Off (paid plan) | On |
| 4 | Loading | Consenter script blocking / click-to-load | After consent | After consent |

## Step 2 — Map the configuration in the Consenter Manager

**Processing location.** Disqus is a US enterprise subject to 18 U.S.C. § 2713 [S6]; list the United States.

| Consenter Manager field | Configuration A — Medium risk | Configuration B — Higher risk (default configuration) |
|---|---|---|
| Processing purposes | Unlock additional website features | Unlock additional website features, Customise online ads |
| Tracking method | Third party tracking (crosssession, cross-website) | Third party tracking (crosssession, cross-website) |
| Identifier | Device identifiers, Authentication-derived identifiers | Device identifiers, Authentication-derived identifiers |
| Data categories | Browsing and interaction data, Device identifiers, IP address, User-provided data, Authentication-derived identifiers | As A, plus Users' profiles |
| Legal role of data recipient | Joint Controller | Joint Controller; ad partners as Individual Controller |
| Personalisation model | No personalisation | Profile based |
| Maximum storage duration | Per Disqus cookie policy | Per Disqus cookie policy |
| Processing location | United States | United States |

**Basis.** Cross-site Disqus account and cookies [S3]; settings [S4][S5]. Joint controllership reflects that the publisher integrates a widget that collects data for Disqus's purposes; confirm with your DPO.
