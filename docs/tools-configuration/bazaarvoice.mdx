> This guide explains how to implement different privacy configurations for Bazaarvoice ratings and reviews and how to reflect them in the Consenter Manager.
>
> 1. Configure the third-party service.
> 2. Map the selected configuration in the Consenter Manager.
> 3. Update your privacy policy to reflect the actual implementation.
>
> The configurations are comparative examples, not guarantees of legal compliance. Verify that the guide matches your product edition, implementation, and current provider documentation.

## At a glance

**Scope.** Bazaarvoice ratings and reviews display (`bv.js`) on the website. Bazaarvoice, Inc. is a US company (Austin, Texas) [S1]. Sources were checked on 16 November 2026.

**What the service does in this scope.** Bazaarvoice states that it cannot create first-party cookies without explicit consent; first-party cookies must be enabled per domain by Bazaarvoice Support, and the website passes consent with the `setConsent` function [S2]. `BVBRANDID` (1 year) and `BVBRANDSID` (session) correlate internal Bazaarvoice web analytics to the same user or session within a client domain [S3]. When the opt-out function is invoked, these cookies are removed and analytics calls go to an anonymous endpoint [S4]. Bazaarvoice's privacy policy states it uses cookies to combine data across multiple websites in the Bazaarvoice Network [S5].

**Configurations included.**

| Configuration | Summary |
|---|---|
| Configuration A — Medium risk | Reviews display without Bazaarvoice cookies (first-party cookies not enabled, or consent `false`); anonymous analytics endpoint. |
| Configuration B — Higher risk | First-party cookies enabled; `setConsent` passes Consenter consent; cookies after consent. |

> ⚠ **Recommendation — Configuration A before consent.** Without cookies the script still loads and sends analytics to Bazaarvoice's anonymous endpoint [S4]. Load the review display after Consenter consent unless your DPO or independent counsel confirms a consent-free basis.

## Step 1 — Configure Bazaarvoice

| # | Configuration area | Where to configure it | Configuration A — Medium risk | Configuration B — Higher risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Processing purposes | Operator decision | Unlock additional website features | Unlock additional website features; Improve the service |
| 2 | First-party cookies | Bazaarvoice Support, per domain [S2] | Not enabled | Enabled |
| 3 | Consent signal | `setConsent({ BVBRANDID: …, BVBRANDSID: … })` [S2] | — | From Consenter |
| 4 | Loading | Consenter script blocking | See ⚠ | After consent |

## Step 2 — Map the configuration in the Consenter Manager

**Processing location.** Bazaarvoice is a US enterprise subject to 18 U.S.C. § 2713 [S6]; list the United States.

| Consenter Manager field | Configuration A — Medium risk | Configuration B — Higher risk |
|---|---|---|
| Processing purposes | Unlock additional website features | Unlock additional website features, Improve the service |
| Tracking method | Third party tracking (single session, cross-website) | First party tracking (cross-session) |
| Identifier | IP address | Device identifiers |
| Data categories | IP address, Browsing and interaction data | Browsing and interaction data, Device identifiers, IP address, Privacy choices |
| Legal role of data recipient | Processor | Processor |
| Personalisation model | No personalisation | No personalisation |
| Maximum storage duration | — | 1 year (`BVBRANDID`) [S3] |
| Processing location | United States | United States |

**Basis.** Cookies [S2][S3]; consent signal [S2], hence "Privacy choices". If the third-party Bazaarvoice Network cookie is active, add "Third party tracking (crosssession, cross-website)" and Individual Controller per the network data use [S5].
