This guide explains how to implement different privacy configurations for Bazaarvoice ratings and reviews and how to reflect them in the Consenter Manager.

1. Configure the third-party service.
2. Map the selected configuration in the Consenter Manager.
3. Update your privacy policy to reflect the actual implementation.

The configurations are comparative examples, not guarantees of legal compliance. Verify that the guide matches your product edition, implementation, and current provider documentation.
</Callout>

## At a glance

**Scope.** Bazaarvoice ratings and reviews display (`bv.js`) on the website. Bazaarvoice, Inc. is a US company (Austin, Texas).

**What the service does in this scope.** Bazaarvoice states that it cannot create first-party cookies without explicit consent; first-party cookies must be enabled per domain by Bazaarvoice Support, and the website passes consent with the `setConsent` function. `BVBRANDID` (1 year) and `BVBRANDSID` (session) correlate internal Bazaarvoice web analytics to the same user or session within a client domain. When the opt-out function is invoked, these cookies are removed and analytics calls go to an anonymous endpoint. Bazaarvoice's privacy policy states it uses cookies to combine data across multiple websites in the Bazaarvoice Network.

**Configurations included.**

| Configuration | Summary |
|---|---|
| Configuration A — Medium risk | Reviews display without Bazaarvoice cookies (first-party cookies not enabled, or consent `false`); anonymous analytics endpoint. |
| Configuration B — Higher risk | First-party cookies enabled; `setConsent` passes Consenter consent; cookies after consent. |

> ⚠ **Recommendation — Configuration A before consent.** Without cookies the script still loads and sends analytics to Bazaarvoice's anonymous endpoint. Load the review display after Consenter consent unless your DPO or independent counsel confirms a consent-free basis.

## Step 1 — Configure Bazaarvoice

| # | Configuration area | Where to configure it | Configuration A — Medium risk | Configuration B — Higher risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Processing purposes | Operator decision | Unlock additional website features | Unlock additional website features; Improve the service |
| 2 | First-party cookies | Bazaarvoice Support, per domain | Not enabled | Enabled |
| 3 | Consent signal | `setConsent({ BVBRANDID: …, BVBRANDSID: … })` | — | From Consenter |
| 4 | Loading | Consenter script blocking | See ⚠ | After consent |

## Step 2 — Map the configuration in the Consenter Manager
