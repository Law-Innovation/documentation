<Callout type="info" title="Overview">
This guide explains how to implement different privacy configurations for Médiamétrie eStat Streaming and how to reflect them in the Consenter Manager.

1. Configure the third-party service.
2. Map the selected configuration in the Consenter Manager.
3. Update your privacy policy to reflect the actual implementation.

The configurations are comparative examples, not guarantees of legal compliance. Verify that the guide matches your product edition, implementation, and current provider documentation.
</Callout>

## At a glance

**Scope.** Médiamétrie eStat Streaming tag for measuring audio and video streaming audiences on websites. Médiamétrie is a French company. Sources were checked on 16 November 2026.

**What the service does in this scope.** The tag supports three `consentType` values: `optin` (measurement with the user's consent), `exempted` (measurement under the CNIL exemption) and `optout` (tag deactivated, no data collected) [S1]. In exempted mode, no UID, IDFA or ADID is sent and no third-party cookie is set; the IP address is used only to determine the country and is then hashed with murmur3 using a monthly-rotating random key, kept for one month and three days; campaign measurement is disabled [S1]. Médiamétrie acts as processor and does not process exempted-mode data for its own purposes [S1].

**Configurations included.**

| Configuration | Summary |
|---|---|
| Configuration A — Lower risk (⚠ no-consent) | `consentType = exempted` for visitors without consent. |
| Configuration B — Medium risk | `consentType = optin` after consent (identifiers and campaign measurement enabled). |

> ⚠ **Recommendation — exemption is self-assessed and France-specific.** CNIL treats audience-measurement tracers as exempt only where they serve solely the publisher's audience measurement, produce anonymous statistics, are not cross-referenced or passed on and do not allow tracking across sites [S2]. Describe A as a **self-assessed exemption**. § 25 TDDDG in Germany contains no equivalent exemption. Run A without consent only after review by your DPO or independent counsel; otherwise set `optout` until consent.

## Step 1 — Configure eStat Streaming

| # | Configuration area | Where to configure it | Configuration A — Lower risk (⚠ no-consent) | Configuration B — Medium risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Processing purposes | Operator decision | Improve the service | Improve the service; Support marketing analytics |
| 2 | `consentType` | Tag configuration, set from the CMP decision [S1] | `exempted` (or `optout` on refusal) | `optin` |
| 3 | Personal data in tagging plan | Must not be included [S1] | Required | Required |
| 4 | Campaign measurement | Automatically disabled in exempted mode [S1] | Off | On |

## Step 2 — Map the configuration in the Consenter Manager

| Consenter Manager field | Configuration A — Lower risk (⚠ no-consent) | Configuration B — Medium risk |
|---|---|---|
| Processing purposes | Improve the service | Improve the service, Support marketing analytics |
| Tracking method | No tracking | First party tracking (cross-session) |
| Identifier | No identifier | Device identifiers |
| Data categories | Aggregated site statistics, Browsing and interaction data, Device characteristics, IP address anonymised | Aggregated site statistics, Browsing and interaction data, Device characteristics, Device identifiers, IP address, Privacy choices |
| Legal role of data recipient | Processor | Processor |
| Personalisation model | No personalisation | No personalisation |
| Maximum storage duration | 1 month and 3 days (hash key) | Per contract |
| Processing location | Not documented | Not documented |

**Basis.** Exempted mode details [S1]. "Privacy choices" is selected in B because the consent mode is passed to Médiamétrie through `consentType` [S1]. B identifiers are inferred from the absence of the exempted-mode restrictions; verify against Médiamétrie's contract annex.
