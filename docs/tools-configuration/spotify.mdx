Typical processing purposes: Unlock additional website features

> This guide explains how to implement different privacy configurations for Spotify content on a website and how to reflect them in the Consenter Manager.
>
> 1. Configure the third-party service.
> 2. Map the selected configuration in the Consenter Manager.
> 3. Update your privacy policy to reflect the actual implementation.
>
> The configurations are comparative examples, not guarantees of legal compliance. Verify that the guide matches your product edition, implementation, and current provider documentation.

## At a glance

**Scope.** Spotify embeds (tracks, playlists, podcasts) via iframe from `open.spotify.com/embed/`, or a plain link. Spotify AB is a Swedish company. Sources were checked on 16 November 2026.

**What the service does in this scope.** Cookies associated with the Spotify embed include `sp_landing`, `sp_ab`, `sp_t`, `sp_usid`, `sp_m` and `_ga` [S1]. When a visitor is logged in to Spotify, the embed attempts to use the visitor's Spotify session [S2].

**Configurations included.**

| Configuration | Summary |
|---|---|
| Configuration A — Lower risk | Link to Spotify; no embed. |
| Configuration B — Higher risk | Embedded player loaded after Consenter consent (or click-to-load). |

**Configuration A without website consent.** A link sets nothing on your website; mention Spotify in the privacy policy.

## Step 1 — Configure Spotify

| # | Configuration area | Where to configure it | Configuration A — Lower risk | Configuration B — Higher risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Processing purposes | Operator decision | — | Unlock additional website features |
| 2 | Integration | Website code | Link | `iframe` embed [S1] |
| 3 | Loading | Consenter script blocking / click-to-load | — | After consent |

## Step 2 — Map the configuration in the Consenter Manager

| Consenter Manager field | Configuration A — Lower risk | Configuration B — Higher risk |
|---|---|---|
| Entry needed | No | Yes |
| Processing purposes | — | Unlock additional website features |
| Tracking method | No tracking | Third party tracking (crosssession, cross-website) |
| Identifier | No identifier | Device identifiers, Authentication-derived identifiers (logged-in visitors) |
| Data categories | — | Browsing and interaction data, Device characteristics, Device identifiers, IP address |
| Legal role of data recipient | — | Individual Controller |
| Personalisation model | — | No personalisation (on your site) |
| Maximum storage duration | — | Per Spotify cookie policy |
| Processing location | — | Sweden / per Spotify privacy policy; check for US transfers |

**Basis.** Embed cookies [S1]; session use [S2]. Spotify processes embed data for its own purposes, hence Individual Controller.
