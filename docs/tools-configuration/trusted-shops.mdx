Typical processing purposes: Unlock additional website features, Receive marketing offers

> This guide explains how to implement different privacy configurations for the Trusted Shops Trustbadge and widgets and how to reflect them in the Consenter Manager.
>
> 1. Configure the third-party service.
> 2. Map the selected configuration in the Consenter Manager.
> 3. Update your privacy policy to reflect the actual implementation.
>
> The configurations are comparative examples, not guarantees of legal compliance. Verify that the guide matches your product edition, implementation, and current provider documentation.

## At a glance

**Scope.** The Trusted Shops Trustbadge and review widgets (Trusted Shops SE, Cologne, Germany). The online shop and Trusted Shops are joint controllers under Art. 26 GDPR [S1][S2]. Sources were checked on 16 November 2026.

**What the service does in this scope.** Trusted Shops states that it does not use cookies when integrating widgets and stores nothing in the visitors' local storage; session cookies are set only when interacting with the #trstd login and Trustbadge AI+ [S3]. Displaying the Trustbadge processes the IP address, which is anonymised immediately, and access data; the Trustbadge is delivered via AWS as CDN on servers in Europe [S4][S5]. In the order process, the Trustbadge can transmit order data with a hashed email address to recognise registered Trusted Shops customers and offer buyer protection and review invites [S6]. With **Enable Trustbadge consent request**, the badge is integrated so that order data is not transmitted automatically [S6].

**Configurations included.**

| Configuration | Summary |
|---|---|
| Configuration A — Lower risk | Trustbadge display only; "Enable Trustbadge consent request" on; no automatic order data transfer. |
| Configuration B — Medium risk | Trustbadge with automatic order data transfer (hashed email, order number) for buyer protection and review invites; after Consenter consent. |

> ⚠ **Recommendation — Configuration A without consent.** Trusted Shops provides privacy-policy text for a variant where consent is not obtained for displaying the Trustbadge [S2]; no cookies are set [S3]. The display still transmits the IP address (immediately anonymised) to Trusted Shops and its CDN [S4]. Have your DPO or independent counsel confirm the legal basis before showing the badge without consent.

## Step 1 — Configure Trusted Shops

| # | Configuration area | Where to configure it | Configuration A — Lower risk | Configuration B — Medium risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Processing purposes | Operator decision | Unlock additional website features | Unlock additional website features; Receive marketing offers |
| 2 | Trustbadge consent request | eTrusted Trustbadge settings [S6] | On | Off (consent via Consenter) |
| 3 | Order data transfer | Checkout integration [S6] | No automatic transfer | Automatic after consent |
| 4 | Privacy policy text | Trusted Shops template [S2] | Variation 2 | Variation 1 |

## Step 2 — Map the configuration in the Consenter Manager

**Processing location.** Servers in Europe [S5]; the CDN provider AWS is a US enterprise subject to 18 U.S.C. § 2713 [S7], so list the United States as a potential access location.

| Consenter Manager field | Configuration A — Lower risk | Configuration B — Medium risk |
|---|---|---|
| Processing purposes | Unlock additional website features | Unlock additional website features, Receive marketing offers |
| Tracking method | No tracking | No tracking |
| Identifier | No identifier | Probabilistic identifiers (hashed email) |
| Data categories | IP address anonymised | IP address anonymised, Probabilistic identifiers, e-commerce Activity |
| Legal role of data recipient | Joint Controller | Joint Controller |
| Personalisation model | No personalisation | No personalisation |
| Maximum storage duration | — (no device storage [S3]) | — (no device storage [S3]) |
| Processing location | EU; United States (potential access) | EU; United States (potential access) |
