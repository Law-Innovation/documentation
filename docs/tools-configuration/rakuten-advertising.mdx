<Callout type="info" title="Overview">
This guide explains how to implement different privacy configurations for the Rakuten Advertising site tracking tag on an advertiser website and how to reflect them in the Consenter Manager.

1. Configure the third-party service.
2. Map the selected configuration in the Consenter Manager.
3. Update your privacy policy to reflect the actual implementation.

The configurations are comparative examples, not guarantees of legal compliance. Verify that the guide matches your product edition, implementation, and current provider documentation.
</Callout>

## At a glance

**Scope.** Rakuten Advertising affiliate site tracking and conversion tag.

**What the service does in this scope.** Rakuten Advertising lists the cookies and storage its customers may use, including `__rmco` and `__rmco_cs` in first-party `localStorage` for consent information. CMP integrations set the `__rmcp2` consent signal for EU/UK visitors; tracking stays restricted until marketing consent is given.

> **Note on consent signals.** Rakuten Advertising expects the `__rmcp2` consent signal from a CMP integration. Consenter does not currently provide this signal, so this guide does not cover configuring it; load the tag only after consent in Consenter. Rakuten Advertising processes digital identifiers such as cookie IDs and may act as a processor under the GDPR in some cases.

**Configurations included.**

| Configuration | Summary |
|---|---|
| Configuration A — Medium risk | Tag loaded only after Consenter consent. |
| Configuration B — Higher risk | Tag fires without a consent signal. |
