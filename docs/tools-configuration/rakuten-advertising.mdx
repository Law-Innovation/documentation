Typical processing purposes: Support marketing analytics

> This guide explains how to implement different privacy configurations for the Rakuten Advertising site tracking tag on an advertiser website and how to reflect them in the Consenter Manager.
>
> 1. Configure the third-party service.
> 2. Map the selected configuration in the Consenter Manager.
> 3. Update your privacy policy to reflect the actual implementation.
>
> The configurations are comparative examples, not guarantees of legal compliance. Verify that the guide matches your product edition, implementation, and current provider documentation.

## At a glance

**Scope.** Rakuten Advertising affiliate site tracking and conversion tag. Sources were checked on 16 November 2026.

**What the service does in this scope.** Rakuten Advertising lists the cookies and storage its customers may use, including `__rmco` and `__rmco_cs` in first-party `localStorage` for consent information [S1]. CMP integrations set the `__rmcp2` consent signal for EU/UK visitors; tracking stays restricted until marketing consent is given [S2]. Rakuten Advertising processes digital identifiers such as cookie IDs and may act as a processor under the GDPR in some cases [S3].

**Configurations included.**

| Configuration | Summary |
|---|---|
| Configuration A — Medium risk | `__rmcp2` consent signal set from Consenter; tracking restricted until consent. |
| Configuration B — Higher risk | Tag fires without a consent signal. |

## Step 1 — Configure Rakuten Advertising

| # | Configuration area | Where to configure it | Configuration A — Medium risk | Configuration B — Higher risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Processing purposes | Operator decision | Support marketing analytics | Support marketing analytics |
| 2 | Consent signal | `__rmcp2` [S2] | Set from Consenter | Not set |
| 3 | Conversion tag | `ranMID` / tracking key | After consent | On page load |

## Step 2 — Map the configuration in the Consenter Manager

**Processing location.** Rakuten Advertising is operated by a US entity of a Japanese group; list the United States (18 U.S.C. § 2713 [S4]).

| Consenter Manager field | Configuration A — Medium risk | Configuration B — Higher risk |
|---|---|---|
| Processing purposes | Support marketing analytics | Support marketing analytics |
| Tracking method | Third party tracking (crosssession, cross-website) | Third party tracking (crosssession, cross-website) |
| Identifier | Device identifiers | Device identifiers |
| Data categories | Device identifiers, Browsing and interaction data, e-commerce Activity, Privacy choices | Device identifiers, Browsing and interaction data, e-commerce Activity |
| Legal role of data recipient | Processor | Processor |
| Personalisation model | No personalisation | No personalisation |
| Maximum storage duration | Per Rakuten cookie policy [S1] | Per Rakuten cookie policy [S1] |
| Processing location | United States / EU | United States / EU |
