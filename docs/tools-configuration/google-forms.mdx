Typical processing purposes: Unlock additional website features

<Callout type="info" title="Overview">
This guide explains how to implement different privacy configurations for Google Forms embedded in a website and how to reflect them in the Consenter Manager.

1. Configure the third-party service.
2. Map the selected configuration in the Consenter Manager.
3. Update your privacy policy to reflect the actual implementation.

The configurations are comparative examples, not guarantees of legal compliance. Verify that the guide matches your product edition, implementation, and current provider documentation.
</Callout>

## At a glance

**Scope.** A Google Form embedded on a website (iframe from `docs.google.com/forms`) or linked from it.

**What the service does in this scope.** The form is rendered by Google and responses are stored in the form owner's Google account. For **Google Workspace** customers, Google Forms is part of the Workspace services; Customer Data is processed under the Cloud Data Processing Addendum, and Workspace In-Scope Editions can select a data region for Forms data at rest ("text, embedded images, responses and draft responses"). A form created with a personal (consumer) Google account is not covered by these terms.

**Configurations included.**

| Configuration | Summary |
|---|---|
| Configuration A — Medium risk | Form owned by a Google Workspace account (Cloud Data Processing Addendum applies), Europe data region selected, email collection off, linked or embedded after consent. |
| Configuration B — Higher risk | Form owned by a personal Google account, embedded on page load after consent, email collection on. |

**Consent.** The embedded iframe loads from a Google domain where the visitor may already have Google cookies. Load the iframe only after consent, or link to the form instead.

| Action | Configuration A — Medium risk | Configuration B — Higher risk |
|---|---|---|
| Account type | Google Workspace | Personal Google account |
| Data region (Forms at rest) | Europe | Not available |
| Collect email addresses | Off | On |
| Embedding | Link, or iframe after consent | Iframe after consent |
| Consenter Manager entries | One entry (Google as Processor) | One entry (Google as Individual Controller) |

## Step 1 — Configure Google Forms

| # | Configuration area | Where to configure it | Configuration A — Medium risk | Configuration B — Higher risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Processing purposes | Operator decision | Unlock additional website features (form submission) | Same |
| 2 | Account type and contract | Google Workspace subscription with Cloud Data Processing Addendum | Workspace | Personal account |
| 3 | Data region | Google Admin console > Data regions (In-Scope Editions only) | Europe | — |
| 4 | Collect email addresses | Form **Settings > Responses > Collect email addresses** | Off | On |
| 5 | Embedding | Website code | Link, or iframe blocked until consent | Iframe blocked until consent |

## Step 2 — Map the configuration in the Consenter Manager

**Processing location.** Data not covered by the Data Regions Policy may be processed wherever Google or its subprocessors maintain facilities. Google LLC is a US company and can be required to disclose data in its possession, custody or control regardless of storage location.

### Configuration A — Medium risk

| Consenter Manager field | Value to select | Basis and conditions |
|---|---|---|
| Processing purposes | Unlock additional website features | Form submission. |
| Tracking method | No tracking | No tracking by the form owner. |
| Identifier | IP address | Transmitted when the iframe loads. |
| Data categories | IP address, Device characteristics, User-provided data | Form responses. Add "direct identifier" if the form asks for names or email addresses. |
| Legal role of data recipient | Processor | Cloud Data Processing Addendum for Workspace Customer Data. |
| Personalisation model | No personalisation | — |
| Maximum storage duration | Until deleted by the form owner | Responses remain until deleted. |
| Processing location | Europe (data at rest); United States as potential processing and access location | — |

### Configuration B — Higher risk

| Consenter Manager field | Value to select | Basis and conditions |
|---|---|---|
| Processing purposes | Unlock additional website features | As A. |
| Tracking method | No tracking | Re-assess if Google cookies are read in the iframe for signed-in visitors. |
| Identifier | IP address, Direct identifier | Email collection on. |
| Data categories | IP address, Device characteristics, User-provided data, direct identifier | — |
| Legal role of data recipient | Individual Controller | No processing terms for personal accounts. |
| Personalisation model | No personalisation | — |
| Maximum storage duration | Until deleted by the form owner | — |
| Processing location | United States as potential processing and access location | — |

## Privacy-policy follow-up

- Google as processor (A) or controller (B); data collected in the form; data region; US transfer and potential access by US authorities.

## Verification checklist

- [ ] No request to `docs.google.com` before consent (if embedded).
- [ ] Form owned by the Workspace account (A); data region set.
