## At a glance

**Scope.** DoubleVerify (DV) verification tags that measure ad impressions (viewability, brand safety, invalid traffic) on a website, either deployed by the publisher or delivered with ads. DoubleVerify, Inc. is a US company.

**What the service does in this scope.** DV states that its technology "does not leverage cookies and does not create profiles or audiences" and measures each ad impression individually without tracking user activity over time. Under DV's data privacy addendum, DV processes personal data as **processor** for the client, while the fraud-elimination part of the solutions is operated by DV as **controller**; DV's processing is limited to pseudonymous information.

**Simplified format.** DV offers no privacy settings for the website operator. The guide uses a single fixed-behaviour configuration.

**Consent.** Verification tags run in the visitor's browser alongside ads. Load publisher-side DV tags together with the advertising they measure, i.e. after advertising consent.

## Step 1 — Configure DoubleVerify

| Configuration area | Fixed behaviour / required action |
|---|---|
| Processing purposes | Support marketing analytics (ad measurement and verification) |

| Roles | Processor; Controller for fraud elimination |
