Typical processing purposes: Support marketing analytics, Customise online ads

<Callout type="info" title="Overview">
This guide explains how to implement different privacy configurations for the TikTok Pixel and how to reflect them in the Consenter Manager.

1. Configure the third-party service.
2. Map the selected configuration in the Consenter Manager.
3. Update your privacy policy to reflect the actual implementation.

The configurations are comparative examples, not guarantees of legal compliance. Verify that the guide matches your product edition, implementation, and current provider documentation.
</Callout>

## At a glance

**Scope.** TikTok Pixel on the advertiser website (Events Manager settings). The Events API is not covered.

**What the service does in this scope.** Third-party cookies are always on for the TikTok Pixel, while first-party cookies are optional and enabled by default; they can be toggled off in the pixel settings in Events Manager. Advanced Matching supplements cookie matching. Where consent is required, TikTok tells advertisers to disable cookie setting and use when a person declines, using a tag manager or CMP. TikTok's consent mode adds `holdConsent`, `grantConsent` and `revokeConsent` to the pixel base code.

**Configurations included.**

| Configuration | Summary |
|---|---|
| Configuration A — Higher risk | Pixel held with `ttq.holdConsent` until Consenter consent; first-party cookies off; no Advanced Matching. |
| Configuration B — Higher risk (default configuration) | Pixel after consent with default cookie settings (first- and third-party cookies). |
| Configuration C — Higher risk | As B, plus Advanced Matching (hashed email/phone). |

## Step 1 — Configure the TikTok Pixel

| # | Configuration area | Where to configure it | Configuration A — Higher risk | Configuration B — Higher risk (default configuration) | Configuration C — Higher risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Processing purposes | Operator decision | Support marketing analytics; Customise online ads | Support marketing analytics; Customise online ads | Support marketing analytics; Customise online ads |
| 2 | Consent gating | `ttq.holdConsent` / `grantConsent` | Hold until Consenter consent | Hold until Consenter consent | Hold until Consenter consent |
| 3 | First-party cookies | Events Manager → pixel settings | Off | On | On |
| 4 | Advanced Matching | Events Manager | Off | Off | On |

## Step 2 — Map the configuration in the Consenter Manager

**Processing location.** Check TikTok's business terms for the contracting entity and third-country transfers, and list the countries named there.

| Consenter Manager field | Configuration A — Higher risk | Configuration B — Higher risk (default configuration) | Configuration C — Higher risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Processing purposes | Support marketing analytics, Customise online ads | Support marketing analytics, Customise online ads | Support marketing analytics, Customise online ads |
| Tracking method | Third party tracking (crosssession, cross-website) | Third party tracking (crosssession, cross-website) | Third party tracking (cross-session, cross-website, cross-device) |
| Identifier | Device identifiers | Device identifiers | Device identifiers, Direct identifier |
| Data categories | Device identifiers, Browsing and interaction data, e-commerce Activity, Users' profiles | Device identifiers, Browsing and interaction data, e-commerce Activity, Users' profiles | Device identifiers, Direct identifier, Browsing and interaction data, e-commerce Activity, Users' profiles |
| Legal role of data recipient | Joint Controller | Joint Controller | Joint Controller |
| Personalisation model | Profile based | Profile based | Profile based |
| Maximum storage duration | Per TikTok cookie list | Per TikTok cookie list | Per TikTok cookie list |
| Processing location | Per TikTok business terms | Per TikTok business terms | Per TikTok business terms |

> **Note on legal role.** The table uses Joint Controller for the collection via the pixel; confirm the role in TikTok's business terms.
