Typical processing purposes: Unlock additional website features

<Callout type="info" title="Overview">
This guide explains how to implement different privacy configurations for embedded Jotform forms and how to reflect them in the Consenter Manager.

1. Configure the third-party service.
2. Map the selected configuration in the Consenter Manager.
3. Update your privacy policy to reflect the actual implementation.

The configurations are comparative examples, not guarantees of legal compliance. Verify that the guide matches your product edition, implementation, and current provider documentation.
</Callout>

## At a glance

**Scope.** Jotform forms embedded on the website (iframe or script). Jotform Inc. is a US company.

**What the service does in this scope.** In the **Data** tab of the account settings, the GDPR / Europe data-centre option moves form data to EU servers in Frankfurt, Germany, and the account is served from `eu.jotform.com`. Jotform's GDPR page states the Frankfurt servers are run by Google. Jotform offers a DPA for EU customers.

**Configurations included.**

| Configuration | Summary |
|---|---|
| Configuration A — Medium risk | EU data centre; form embedded and loaded after Consenter consent. |
| Configuration B — Higher risk (default configuration) | US data centre; form embedded after consent. |

**No configuration without prior consent.** Embedded forms load from Jotform and may set cookies on Jotform's domain; Jotform's sources do not list them. Verify in the browser.

## Step 1 — Configure Jotform

| # | Configuration area | Where to configure it | Configuration A — Medium risk | Configuration B — Higher risk (default configuration) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Processing purposes | Operator decision | Unlock additional website features | Unlock additional website features |
| 2 | Data centre | **Account settings > Data** | Europe (Frankfurt) | US (default) |
| 3 | Loading | Consenter script blocking | After consent | After consent |
| 4 | DPA | Jotform GDPR page | Signed | Signed |

## Step 2 — Map the configuration in the Consenter Manager

**Processing location.** Jotform is a US enterprise, and the EU servers are operated by Google; both are subject to the US CLOUD Act. List the United States in all configurations.

| Consenter Manager field | Configuration A — Medium risk | Configuration B — Higher risk (default configuration) |
|---|---|---|
| Processing purposes | Unlock additional website features | Unlock additional website features |
| Tracking method | Third party tracking (single session, cross-website) | Third party tracking (single session, cross-website) |
| Identifier | No identifier | No identifier |
| Data categories | IP address, Device characteristics, User-provided data | IP address, Device characteristics, User-provided data |
| Legal role of data recipient | Processor | Processor |
| Personalisation model | No personalisation | No personalisation |
| Maximum storage duration | Per form retention settings | Per form retention settings |
| Processing location | Germany; United States | United States |
