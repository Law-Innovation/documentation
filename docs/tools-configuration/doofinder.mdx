<Callout type="info" title="Overview">
This guide explains how to implement different privacy configurations for Doofinder site search and how to reflect them in the Consenter Manager.

1. Configure the third-party service.
2. Map the selected configuration in the Consenter Manager.
3. Update your privacy policy to reflect the actual implementation.

The configurations are comparative examples, not guarantees of legal compliance. Verify that the guide matches your product edition, implementation, and current provider documentation.
</Callout>

## At a glance

**Scope.** Doofinder search layer, recommendations and Quiz Maker (Doofinder S.L., Madrid, Spain).

**What the service does in this scope.** Doofinder uses `localStorage` instead of cookies, including a unique random ID that tracks interactions on the website for 24 hours per session when the script loads. Storage keys include `df-random-userid` (analytical), `df-session` (functional) and `df-cookies-allowed`. The **Cookie Consent for Full Product Functionality** toggle (**Configuration > General Settings > Overview**) enables consent validation; the site then calls `Doofinder.enableCookies` once the user consents, and without consent latest searches and session-based data such as `user_id` are unavailable.

**Configurations included.**

| Configuration | Summary |
|---|---|
| Configuration A — Medium risk | Consent validation on; `Doofinder.enableCookies` called from Consenter; search works with reduced functionality before consent. |
| Configuration B — Higher risk (default configuration) | Consent validation off; `localStorage` IDs written on script load. |

> ⚠ **Note on `localStorage`.** Doofinder suggests `localStorage` is not strictly regulated like cookies. Under § 25 TDDDG, storing and reading information on the device is regulated regardless of the technology. Use A, and have your DPO confirm whether the functional entries written before consent in A are strictly necessary.

## Step 1 — Configure Doofinder

| # | Configuration area | Where to configure it | Configuration A — Medium risk | Configuration B — Higher risk (default configuration) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Processing purposes | Operator decision | Unlock additional website features; Improve the service | Unlock additional website features; Improve the service |
| 2 | Cookie consent validation | **Configuration > General Settings > Overview** | On | Off |
| 3 | Consent signal | `Doofinder.enableCookies` | On Consenter consent | — |

## Step 2 — Map the configuration in the Consenter Manager

**Processing location.** Doofinder is a Spanish company; check its DPA for US sub-processors and list the United States if present.

| Consenter Manager field | Configuration A — Medium risk | Configuration B — Higher risk (default configuration) |
|---|---|---|
| Processing purposes | Unlock additional website features, Improve the service | Unlock additional website features, Improve the service |
| Tracking method | First party tracking (single session) | First party tracking (single session) |
