<Callout type="info" title="Overview">
This guide explains how to implement different privacy configurations for Dynamic Yield and how to reflect them in the Consenter Manager.

1. Configure the third-party service.
2. Map the selected configuration in the Consenter Manager.
3. Update your privacy policy to reflect the actual implementation.

The configurations are comparative examples, not guarantees of legal compliance. Verify that the guide matches your product edition, implementation, and current provider documentation.
</Callout>

## At a glance

**Scope.** The Dynamic Yield (Mastercard) client-side script for personalisation, testing and recommendations.

**What the service does in this scope.** Cookies include `_dyid` (unique identifier, customer's domain), `_dyid_server` (1 year, customer's domain), `_dyjsession` (session) and `DYID` / `DYSES` on the `dynamicyield.com` domain. Dynamic Yield states it does not track users across multiple sites. With **Active Cookie Consent** enabled, users are not served personalised experiences unless they actively consent, and no personal data is processed for personalisation without consent except from strictly necessary cookies; consent is passed with `DY.userActiveConsent = { accepted: true|false }`. Non-personalised campaigns can be served to users without consent. Dynamic Yield operates US and EU data centres.

**Configurations included.**

| Configuration | Summary |
|---|---|
| Configuration A — Medium risk | Active Cookie Consent on; EU data centre; personalisation only after consent; non-personalised campaigns otherwise. |
| Configuration B — Higher risk | Script loaded after consent; full personalisation, recommendations and audience targeting; US data centre. |

> ⚠ **Recommendation — script before consent.** In A the script runs before consent and Dynamic Yield still processes data "stemming from strictly necessary cookies". Whether its cookies qualify as strictly necessary under § 25(2) TDDDG is not established by the sources. Block the script in Consenter until consent unless your DPO or independent counsel confirms otherwise.

## Step 1 — Configure Dynamic Yield

| # | Configuration area | Where to configure it | Configuration A — Medium risk | Configuration B — Higher risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Processing purposes | Operator decision | Personalise the website | Personalise the website; Receive personalised marketing offers |
| 2 | Active Cookie Consent | Implement `DY.userActiveConsent`; preview in **Settings > Data Privacy > Active Cookie Consent**; activation by Support | On | On |
| 3 | Non-personalised campaigns for non-consenting users | **Settings > Privacy Center** | On | — |
| 4 | Data centre | Account setup (US or EU) | EU | US |
| 5 | Loading | Consenter | See ⚠ recommendation | After consent |

## Step 2 — Map the configuration in the Consenter Manager

**Processing location.** Dynamic Yield belongs to Mastercard, a US enterprise subject to the US CLOUD Act; list the United States as a potential access location in all configurations.

| Consenter Manager field | Configuration A — Medium risk | Configuration B — Higher risk |
|---|---|---|
| Processing purposes | Personalise the website | Personalise the website, Receive personalised marketing offers |
| Tracking method | First party tracking (cross-session) | Third party tracking (crosssession, cross-website) |
| Identifier | Device identifiers | Device identifiers |
| Data categories | Browsing and interaction data, Device identifiers, Device characteristics, Privacy choices, Users' profiles | Browsing and interaction data, Device identifiers, Device characteristics, e-commerce Activity, Users' profiles |
| Legal role of data recipient | Processor | Processor |
| Personalisation model | Profile based | Profile based |
| Maximum storage duration | 1 year (`_dyid_server`) | 1 year (`_dyid_server`, `DYID`) |
| Processing location | EU data centre; United States as potential access location | United States |
