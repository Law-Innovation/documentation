Typical processing purposes: Personalise the website, Receive personalised marketing offers

> This guide explains how to implement different privacy configurations for Dynamic Yield and how to reflect them in the Consenter Manager.
>
> 1. Configure the third-party service.
> 2. Map the selected configuration in the Consenter Manager.
> 3. Update your privacy policy to reflect the actual implementation.
>
> The configurations are comparative examples, not guarantees of legal compliance. Verify that the guide matches your product edition, implementation, and current provider documentation.

## At a glance

**Scope.** The Dynamic Yield (Mastercard) client-side script for personalisation, testing and recommendations. Sources were checked on 16 November 2026.

**What the service does in this scope.** Cookies include `_dyid` (unique identifier, customer's domain), `_dyid_server` (1 year, customer's domain), `_dyjsession` (session) and `DYID` / `DYSES` on the `dynamicyield.com` domain [S1]. Dynamic Yield states it does not track users across multiple sites [S2]. With **Active Cookie Consent** enabled, users are not served personalised experiences unless they actively consent, and no personal data is processed for personalisation without consent except from strictly necessary cookies; consent is passed with `DY.userActiveConsent = { accepted: true|false }` [S3][S4]. Non-personalised campaigns can be served to users without consent [S5]. Dynamic Yield operates US and EU data centres [S5].

**Configurations included.**

| Configuration | Summary |
|---|---|
| Configuration A — Medium risk | Active Cookie Consent on; EU data centre; personalisation only after consent; non-personalised campaigns otherwise. |
| Configuration B — Higher risk | Script loaded after consent; full personalisation, recommendations and audience targeting; US data centre. |

> ⚠ **Recommendation — script before consent.** In A the script runs before consent and Dynamic Yield still processes data "stemming from strictly necessary cookies" [S3]. Whether its cookies qualify as strictly necessary under § 25(2) TDDDG is not established by the sources. Block the script in Consenter until consent unless your DPO or independent counsel confirms otherwise.

## Step 1 — Configure Dynamic Yield

| # | Configuration area | Where to configure it | Configuration A — Medium risk | Configuration B — Higher risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Processing purposes | Operator decision | Personalise the website | Personalise the website; Receive personalised marketing offers |
| 2 | Active Cookie Consent | Implement `DY.userActiveConsent`; preview in **Settings > Data Privacy > Active Cookie Consent**; activation by Support [S4] | On | On |
| 3 | Non-personalised campaigns for non-consenting users | **Settings > Privacy Center** [S5] | On | — |
| 4 | Data centre | Account setup (US or EU) [S5] | EU | US |
| 5 | Loading | Consenter | See ⚠ recommendation | After consent |

## Step 2 — Map the configuration in the Consenter Manager

**Processing location.** Dynamic Yield belongs to Mastercard, a US enterprise subject to 18 U.S.C. § 2713 [S6]; list the United States as a potential access location in all configurations.

| Consenter Manager field | Configuration A — Medium risk | Configuration B — Higher risk |
|---|---|---|
| Processing purposes | Personalise the website | Personalise the website, Receive personalised marketing offers |
| Tracking method | First party tracking (cross-session) | Third party tracking (crosssession, cross-website) |
| Identifier | Device identifiers | Device identifiers |
| Data categories | Browsing and interaction data, Device identifiers, Device characteristics, Privacy choices, Users' profiles | Browsing and interaction data, Device identifiers, Device characteristics, e-commerce Activity, Users' profiles |
| Legal role of data recipient | Processor | Processor |
| Personalisation model | Profile based | Profile based |
| Maximum storage duration | 1 year (`_dyid_server`) | 1 year (`_dyid_server`, `DYID`) |
| Processing location | EU data centre; United States as potential access location | United States |

**Basis.** Cookies and domains [S1]; consent signal transmitted [S4], hence "Privacy choices" in A. In B, `DYID` is set on `dynamicyield.com` [S1]; although Dynamic Yield states it does not track users across sites [S2], a cookie on its own domain is technically readable across customer sites — verify and adjust the tracking method if Dynamic Yield confirms per-site isolation.
