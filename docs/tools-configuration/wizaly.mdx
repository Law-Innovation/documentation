Typical processing purposes: Support marketing analytics

<Callout type="info" title="Overview">
This guide explains how to implement different privacy configurations for Wizaly and how to reflect them in the Consenter Manager.

1. Configure the third-party service.
2. Map the selected configuration in the Consenter Manager.
3. Update your privacy policy to reflect the actual implementation.

The configurations are comparative examples, not guarantees of legal compliance. Verify that the guide matches your product edition, implementation, and current provider documentation.
</Callout>

## At a glance

**Scope.** Wizaly marketing attribution tracking in **hybrid mode**. Wizaly is a French company. Sources were checked on 16 November 2026.

**What the service does in this scope.** With consent, Wizaly collects individualised, detailed data and stores a `wzuid` entry in local storage; without consent, it collects aggregated data only, sets no persistent cookie, limits IP-based geolocation to country level, keeps no personal data and allows only aggregated viewing and export [S1]. The opt-out cookie `esv_opt_out` (one year) makes the pixel ignore calls [S1]. Wizaly acts as processor and keeps each client's data in a separate database [S1]. Servers are hosted by IBM France; Amazon Web Services EMEA collects and forwards browsing traces through a node in France; Wizaly commits not to transfer EU personal data outside the EU [S1].

**Configurations included.**

| Configuration | Summary |
|---|---|
| Configuration A — Lower risk (⚠ no-consent) | Collection without consent: aggregated, no persistent cookie, country-level location. |
| Configuration B — Higher risk | Collection with consent: `wzuid` in local storage, individual-level attribution data. |

> ⚠ **Recommendation — exemption is self-assessed and France-specific.** CNIL treats audience-measurement tracers as exempt only under strict conditions [S2]. Describe A as a **self-assessed exemption**. § 25 TDDDG in Germany contains no equivalent exemption. Run A without consent only after review by your DPO or independent counsel.

## Step 1 — Configure Wizaly

| # | Configuration area | Where to configure it | Configuration A — Lower risk (⚠ no-consent) | Configuration B — Higher risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Processing purposes | Operator decision | Support marketing analytics | Support marketing analytics |
| 2 | Consent status | Passed to Wizaly with each record from the CMP [S1] | Refused / not expressed | Granted |
| 3 | Opt-out | Wizaly opt-out page (iframe, CNAME recommended) or `esv_opt_out` cookie [S1] | Provide | Provide |

## Step 2 — Map the configuration in the Consenter Manager

**Processing location.** France (IBM France; AWS node in France) [S1]. IBM and Amazon are US enterprises subject to 18 U.S.C. § 2713 [S3]; list the United States as a potential access location.

| Consenter Manager field | Configuration A — Lower risk (⚠ no-consent) | Configuration B — Higher risk |
|---|---|---|
| Processing purposes | Support marketing analytics | Support marketing analytics |
| Tracking method | No tracking | First party tracking (cross-session) |
| Identifier | No identifier | Device identifiers |
| Data categories | Aggregated site statistics, Browsing and interaction data, Non-precise location data | Browsing and interaction data, Device identifiers, Device characteristics, IP address, Users' profiles, Privacy choices |
| Legal role of data recipient | Processor | Processor |
| Personalisation model | No personalisation | No personalisation |
| Maximum storage duration | Aggregates only | Personal data deleted within 90 days after account closure [S1] |
| Processing location | France; United States as potential access location | Same |

**Basis.** Hybrid mode description [S1]. "Privacy choices" in B because consent status is stored with each record [S1].
