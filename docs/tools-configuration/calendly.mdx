<Callout type="info" title="Overview">
This guide explains how to implement different privacy configurations for an embedded Calendly scheduler and how to reflect them in the Consenter Manager.

1. Configure the third-party service.
2. Map the selected configuration in the Consenter Manager.
3. Update your privacy policy to reflect the actual implementation.

The configurations are comparative examples, not guarantees of legal compliance. Verify that the guide matches your product edition, implementation, and current provider documentation.
</Callout>

## At a glance

**Scope.** Calendly inline, pop-up and badge embeds on the website. Calendly LLC is a US company. Sources were checked on 16 November 2026.

**What the service does in this scope.** Calendly shows its own cookie banner in embedded pages by default; it can be hidden with `hide_gdpr_banner=1`. Calendly uses cookies even if the banner is hidden, and recommends waiting to load Calendly until users accept performance cookies [S1].

**Configurations included.**

| Configuration | Summary |
|---|---|
| Configuration A — Medium risk | Link to the Calendly booking page (no embed on the website). |
| Configuration B — Higher risk | Inline or pop-up embed loaded after Consenter consent; Calendly banner hidden. |

**Configuration A without website consent.** A plain link sets nothing on your website; Calendly handles cookies on its own domain. No Consenter entry is required, but mention Calendly in the privacy policy.

## Step 1 — Configure Calendly

| # | Configuration area | Where to configure it | Configuration A — Medium risk | Configuration B — Higher risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Processing purposes | Operator decision | — (external link) | Unlock additional website features |
| 2 | Integration | Website code | Link to `calendly.com/...` | Embed code with `widget.js` |
| 3 | Calendly cookie banner | `hide_gdpr_banner=1` [S1] | — | Hidden (Consenter handles consent) |
| 4 | Loading | Consenter script blocking [S1] | — | After consent |

## Step 2 — Map the configuration in the Consenter Manager

**Processing location.** Calendly is a US enterprise subject to 18 U.S.C. § 2713 [S2]; list the United States.

| Consenter Manager field | Configuration A — Medium risk | Configuration B — Higher risk |
|---|---|---|
| Entry needed | No | Yes |
| Processing purposes | — | Unlock additional website features |
| Tracking method | No tracking | Third party tracking (crosssession, cross-website) |
| Identifier | No identifier | Device identifiers |
| Data categories | — | Browsing and interaction data, Device characteristics, Device identifiers, IP address, User-provided data, direct identifier |
| Legal role of data recipient | — | Processor |
| Personalisation model | — | No personalisation |
| Maximum storage duration | — | Per Calendly cookie notice |
| Processing location | — | United States |

**Basis.** Embed cookies [S1]. The third-party mapping in B reflects that the embed runs in an iframe on Calendly's domain, where its cookies are set; verify in the browser.
