Typical processing purposes: Support marketing analytics, Receive personalised marketing offers

> This guide explains how to implement different privacy configurations for ActiveCampaign site tracking and how to reflect them in the Consenter Manager.
>
> 1. Configure the third-party service.
> 2. Map the selected configuration in the Consenter Manager.
> 3. Update your privacy policy to reflect the actual implementation.
>
> The configurations are comparative examples, not guarantees of legal compliance. Verify that the guide matches your product edition, implementation, and current provider documentation.

## At a glance

**Scope.** ActiveCampaign forms and the Site Tracking code. ActiveCampaign, LLC is a US company. Sources were checked on 16 November 2026.

**What the service does in this scope.** Site Tracking uses first-party cookies to track visits to every page with the tracking code; contacts are identified after submitting an ActiveCampaign form (with redirect to a tracked page) or via `vgo('setEmail', …)` [S1]. For consent, ActiveCampaign instructs changing `vgo('setTrackByDefault', true)` to `false` and calling `vgo('process', 'allowTracking')` after the visitor agrees [S2].

**Configurations included.**

| Configuration | Summary |
|---|---|
| Configuration A — Medium risk | Forms only; Site Tracking not installed. |
| Configuration B — Higher risk | Site Tracking with `setTrackByDefault(false)`; `allowTracking` after Consenter consent; contacts identified. |

**Default behaviour.** The standard snippet contains `setTrackByDefault(true)` [S2]; this is not suitable for EU visitors without prior consent.

## Step 1 — Configure ActiveCampaign

| # | Configuration area | Where to configure it | Configuration A — Medium risk | Configuration B — Higher risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Processing purposes | Operator decision | Receive marketing offers (form) | Support marketing analytics; Receive personalised marketing offers |
| 2 | Site Tracking | **Website > Site Tracking**; snippet [S1] | Off | On |
| 3 | Track by default | `vgo('setTrackByDefault', …)` [S2] | — | `false` |
| 4 | Consent signal | `vgo('process', 'allowTracking')` on Consenter consent [S2] | — | Yes |
| 5 | Contact identification | Form redirect / `vgo('setEmail', …)` [S1] | — | Yes |

## Step 2 — Map the configuration in the Consenter Manager

**Processing location.** ActiveCampaign is a US enterprise subject to 18 U.S.C. § 2713 [S3]; list the United States.

| Consenter Manager field | Configuration A — Medium risk | Configuration B — Higher risk |
|---|---|---|
| Processing purposes | Receive marketing offers | Support marketing analytics, Receive personalised marketing offers |
| Tracking method | No tracking | First party tracking (cross-session) |
| Identifier | Direct identifier (on submission) | Device identifiers, Direct identifier |
| Data categories | User-provided data, direct identifier | Browsing and interaction data, Device identifiers, User-provided data, direct identifier, Users' profiles, Privacy choices |
| Legal role of data recipient | Processor | Processor |
| Personalisation model | No personalisation | Profile based |
| Maximum storage duration | Per contact retention | Per cookie inspection |
| Processing location | United States | United States |

**Basis.** First-party cookies and identification [S1]; consent signal via `allowTracking` [S2], hence "Privacy choices".
