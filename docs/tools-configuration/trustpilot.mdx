<Callout type="info" title="Overview">
This guide explains how to implement different privacy configurations for Trustpilot on a website and how to reflect them in the Consenter Manager.

1. Configure the third-party service.
2. Map the selected configuration in the Consenter Manager.
3. Update your privacy policy to reflect the actual implementation.

The configurations are comparative examples, not guarantees of legal compliance. Verify that the guide matches your product edition, implementation, and current provider documentation.
</Callout>

## At a glance

**Scope.** Trustpilot TrustBox widgets and the invitation script (Trustpilot A/S, Copenhagen, Denmark).

**What the service does in this scope.** TrustBox widgets are embeddable components that display a business's Trustpilot rating, from star ratings to review carousels. Widgets are loaded from `widget.trustpilot.com`. The Trustpilot invitation script transmits customer data (name, email, order reference) to Trustpilot to send review invitations. Trustpilot's cookie policy describes its own cookies.

**Configurations included.**

| Configuration | Summary |
|---|---|
| Configuration A — Medium risk | TrustBox widget loaded after Consenter consent. |
| Configuration B — Higher risk | TrustBox plus invitation script transmitting order data, after consent. |

## Step 1 — Configure Trustpilot

| # | Configuration area | Where to configure it | Configuration A — Medium risk | Configuration B — Higher risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Processing purposes | Operator decision | Unlock additional website features | Unlock additional website features |
| 2 | TrustBox | Trustpilot Business / plugin | On, after consent | On, after consent |
| 3 | Invitation script | Integrations | Off | On, after consent |

## Step 2 — Map the configuration in the Consenter Manager

**Processing location.** Trustpilot A/S is Danish; check its privacy policy for US sub-processors and list the United States if present.

| Consenter Manager field | Configuration A — Medium risk | Configuration B — Higher risk |
|---|---|---|
| Processing purposes | Unlock additional website features | Unlock additional website features |
| Tracking method | Third party tracking (crosssession, cross-website) | Third party tracking (crosssession, cross-website) |
| Identifier | Device identifiers | Device identifiers, Direct identifier |
| Data categories | Browsing and interaction data, Device characteristics, IP address | As A, plus direct identifier, e-commerce Activity |
| Legal role of data recipient | Individual Controller | Individual Controller |
| Personalisation model | No personalisation | No personalisation |
| Maximum storage duration | Per Trustpilot cookie policy | Per Trustpilot cookie policy |
| Processing location | Denmark; per privacy policy | Denmark; per privacy policy |
