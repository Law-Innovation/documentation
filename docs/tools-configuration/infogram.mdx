Typical processing purposes: Unlock additional website features

<Callout type="info" title="Overview">
This guide explains how to implement different privacy configurations for Infogram charts on a website and how to reflect them in the Consenter Manager.

1. Configure the third-party service.
2. Map the selected configuration in the Consenter Manager.
3. Update your privacy policy to reflect the actual implementation.

The configurations are comparative examples, not guarantees of legal compliance. Verify that the guide matches your product edition, implementation, and current provider documentation.
</Callout>

## At a glance

**Scope.** Infogram charts, maps and infographics (Infogram SIA, Latvia, part of Prezi Inc., US).

**What the service does in this scope.** Prezi and Infogram use cookies, including cookies placed by advertising networks with Prezi's permission. Prezi participates in the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

**Configurations included.**

| Configuration | Summary |
|---|---|
| Configuration A — Lower risk | Static image export of the chart hosted on your site; no embed. |
| Configuration B — Higher risk | Interactive Infogram embed loaded after Consenter consent / click-to-load. |

## Step 1 — Configure Infogram

| # | Configuration area | Where to configure it | Configuration A — Lower risk | Configuration B — Higher risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Processing purposes | Operator decision | — | Unlock additional website features |
| 2 | Integration | Infogram share / download | Image download, self-hosted | Embed code |
| 3 | Loading | Consenter script blocking | — | After consent |

## Step 2 — Map the configuration in the Consenter Manager

**Processing location.** Prezi Inc. is a US enterprise subject to the US CLOUD Act; list the United States.

| Consenter Manager field | Configuration A — Lower risk | Configuration B — Higher risk |
|---|---|---|
| Entry needed | No | Yes |
| Processing purposes | — | Unlock additional website features |
| Tracking method | No tracking | Third party tracking (crosssession, cross-website) |
| Identifier | No identifier | Device identifiers |
| Data categories | — | Browsing and interaction data, Device characteristics, Device identifiers, IP address |
| Legal role of data recipient | — | Individual Controller |
| Personalisation model | — | No personalisation |
| Maximum storage duration | — | Per Infogram cookie policy |
| Processing location | — | Latvia; United States |
