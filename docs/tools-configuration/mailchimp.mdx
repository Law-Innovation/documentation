<Callout type="info" title="Overview">
This guide explains how to implement different privacy configurations for Mailchimp on a website and how to reflect them in the Consenter Manager.

1. Configure the third-party service.
2. Map the selected configuration in the Consenter Manager.
3. Update your privacy policy to reflect the actual implementation.

The configurations are comparative examples, not guarantees of legal compliance. Verify that the guide matches your product edition, implementation, and current provider documentation.
</Callout>

## At a glance

**Scope.** Mailchimp sign-up forms and the Connected Site snippet on the customer's website. Mailchimp is part of Intuit Inc., a US company. Email open and click tracking is outside the scope of the cookie banner.

**What the service does in this scope.** Mailchimp acts as a data processor for its users. The Connected Site snippet allows cookies, pixels and other technologies to be set on the member's connected site to facilitate certain automations. Mailchimp offers optional GDPR fields on sign-up forms with opt-in checkboxes.

**Configurations included.**

| Configuration | Summary |
|---|---|
| Configuration A — Medium risk | Embedded sign-up form only (no Connected Site snippet); after consent. |
| Configuration B — Higher risk | Connected Site snippet with site tracking and pop-up forms; after consent. |

**No configuration without prior consent in this guide.** Mailchimp does not publish which cookies the embedded form sets; verify in the browser. If an embedded form demonstrably sets nothing on the device, it may be loaded on the form's own legal basis after DPO review.

## Step 1 — Configure Mailchimp

| # | Configuration area | Where to configure it | Configuration A — Medium risk | Configuration B — Higher risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Processing purposes | Operator decision | Receive marketing offers | Receive marketing offers; Receive personalised marketing offers |
| 2 | Connected Site snippet | **Website > Connected sites** | Not installed | Installed, loaded after consent |
| 3 | Sign-up form | Form builder; GDPR fields | Embedded form with GDPR fields | Pop-up form via Connected Site |

## Step 2 — Map the configuration in the Consenter Manager

**Processing location.** Intuit is a US enterprise; Mailchimp processes data in the United States.

| Consenter Manager field | Configuration A — Medium risk | Configuration B — Higher risk |
|---|---|---|
| Processing purposes | Receive marketing offers | Receive marketing offers, Receive personalised marketing offers |
| Tracking method | No tracking | First party tracking (cross-session) |
| Identifier | Direct identifier (on submission) | Device identifiers, Direct identifier |
| Data categories | User-provided data, direct identifier | Browsing and interaction data, Device identifiers, e-commerce Activity, User-provided data, direct identifier, Users' profiles |
| Legal role of data recipient | Processor | Processor |
| Personalisation model | No personalisation | Profile based |
| Maximum storage duration | Per audience retention | Per cookie inspection |
| Processing location | United States | United States |
