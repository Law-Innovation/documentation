Typical processing purposes: Support marketing analytics, Receive personalised marketing offers

<Callout type="info" title="Overview">
This guide explains how to implement different privacy configurations for Mautic and how to reflect them in the Consenter Manager.

1. Configure the third-party service.
2. Map the selected configuration in the Consenter Manager.
3. Update your privacy policy to reflect the actual implementation.

The configurations are comparative examples, not guarantees of legal compliance. Verify that the guide matches your product edition, implementation, and current provider documentation.
</Callout>

## At a glance

**Scope.** Mautic open-source marketing automation: the `mtc.js` tracking script on the website and Mautic forms. Sources were checked on 16 November 2026.

**What the service does in this scope.** The tracking script is loaded from the Mautic instance (`https://<your-mautic>/mtc.js`) [S1]. Mautic sets the first-party cookies `mautic_device_id`, `mtc_id`, `mautic_referer_id` and `mtc_sid` [S2]; Mautic's own site lists `mautic_device_id` with 12 months [S3]. Mautic merges actions into the contact with the same cookie or creates one based on the device ID [S4]. The tracking pixel (`mtracking.gif`) in emails and landing pages can set cookies without checking consent [S2].

**Configurations included.**

| Configuration | Summary |
|---|---|
| Configuration A — Medium risk | Self-hosted Mautic in the EU; `mtc.js` loaded after consent; anonymous visits and form-identified contacts. |
| Configuration B — Higher risk | Mautic hosted by a third party (e.g. Mautic SaaS partner); `mtc.js` after consent; contact tracking linked to email clicks. |

**No configuration without prior consent.** All four cookies are set by `mtc.js` [S2]. Note that Mautic landing pages and tracking pixels can set cookies independently of the website script [S2][S5]; block or configure these as well.

## Step 1 — Configure Mautic

| # | Configuration area | Where to configure it | Configuration A — Medium risk | Configuration B — Higher risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Processing purposes | Operator decision | Support marketing analytics | Support marketing analytics; Receive personalised marketing offers |
| 2 | Hosting | Operator infrastructure | Self-hosted (EU) | Third-party host |
| 3 | Tracking script | `mtc.js` via Consenter script blocking [S1] | After consent | After consent |
| 4 | Mautic landing pages / tracking pixel | Mautic configuration | Avoid on pages without consent [S5] | Used in emails |

## Step 2 — Map the configuration in the Consenter Manager

| Consenter Manager field | Configuration A — Medium risk | Configuration B — Higher risk |
|---|---|---|
| Processing purposes | Support marketing analytics | Support marketing analytics, Receive personalised marketing offers |
| Tracking method | First party tracking (cross-session) | First party tracking (cross-session) |
| Identifier | Device identifiers, Direct identifier (after form submission) | Device identifiers, Direct identifier |
| Data categories | Browsing and interaction data, Device characteristics, Device identifiers, IP address, User-provided data | As A, plus direct identifier, Users' profiles |
| Legal role of data recipient | Self-hosted | Processor |
| Personalisation model | Group based (behaviour) | Profile based |
| Maximum storage duration | 12 months (`mautic_device_id`, per Mautic's own site [S3]) | 12 months |
| Processing location | Own EU server | Per hosting contract; add the United States if a US provider hosts the instance |
