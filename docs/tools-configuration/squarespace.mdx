Typical processing purposes: Improve the service

<Callout type="info" title="Overview">
This guide explains how to implement different privacy configurations for Squarespace Analytics and how to reflect them in the Consenter Manager. Hosting and strictly necessary cookies (such as `crumb`) are out of scope.

1. Configure the third-party service.
2. Map the selected configuration in the Consenter Manager.
3. Update your privacy policy to reflect the actual implementation.

The configurations are comparative examples, not guarantees of legal compliance. Verify that the guide matches your product edition, implementation, and current provider documentation.
</Callout>

## At a glance

**Scope.** Squarespace's built-in visitor analytics. Google Analytics or pixels added via Code Injection need their own entries. Sources were checked on 16 November 2026.

**What the service does in this scope.** Squarespace's cookie settings live under **Website > Website Tools > Cookies & Visitor Data**; with the banner on and cookies restricted, non-essential Squarespace Analytics or Google Analytics cookies are not dropped until the visitor accepts [S1]. Squarespace counts site-analytics cookies among those needing consent [S2]. The Squarespace banner governs Squarespace Analytics; a third-party banner does not control it [S3].

**Configurations included.**

| Configuration | Summary |
|---|---|
| Configuration A — Medium risk | Cookies restricted under Cookies & Visitor Data; Squarespace Analytics cookies only after acceptance. |
| Configuration B — Higher risk | Cookies not restricted; Squarespace Analytics cookies set on page load. |

> ⚠ **Recommendation.** Because a third-party banner cannot control Squarespace Analytics [S3], keep the restriction enabled when Consenter is your banner and check with your DPO how the two consent layers interact.

## Step 1 — Configure Squarespace

| # | Configuration area | Where to configure it | Configuration A — Medium risk | Configuration B — Higher risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Processing purposes | Operator decision | Improve the service | Improve the service |
| 2 | Cookie restriction | **Website > Website Tools > Cookies & Visitor Data** [S1] | Restricted until acceptance | Not restricted |

## Step 2 — Map the configuration in the Consenter Manager

**Processing location.** Squarespace is a US enterprise; list the United States (18 U.S.C. § 2713 [S4]).

| Consenter Manager field | Configuration A — Medium risk | Configuration B — Higher risk |
|---|---|---|
| Processing purposes | Improve the service | Improve the service |
| Tracking method | First party tracking (cross-session) | First party tracking (cross-session) |
| Identifier | Device identifiers | Device identifiers |
| Data categories | Aggregated site statistics, Browsing and interaction data, Device identifiers, Device characteristics | Aggregated site statistics, Browsing and interaction data, Device identifiers, Device characteristics |
| Legal role of data recipient | Processor | Processor |
| Personalisation model | No personalisation | No personalisation |
| Maximum storage duration | Per Squarespace cookie list | Per Squarespace cookie list |
| Processing location | United States | United States |
