Typical processing purposes: Unlock additional website features

<Callout type="info" title="Overview">
This guide explains how to implement different privacy configurations for Stripe.js and its advanced fraud detection signals and how to reflect them in the Consenter Manager. Processing a payment the visitor has chosen is out of scope.

1. Configure the third-party service.
2. Map the selected configuration in the Consenter Manager.
3. Update your privacy policy to reflect the actual implementation.

The configurations are comparative examples, not guarantees of legal compliance. Verify that the guide matches your product edition, implementation, and current provider documentation.
</Callout>

## At a glance

**Scope.** Stripe.js (Elements, Checkout) on the merchant website.

**What the service does in this scope.** Stripe.js provides advanced fraud detection based on signals about device characteristics and user activity; collection is disclosed in Stripe's privacy and cookie policies. The signals are sent to Stripe via periodic requests to `m.stripe.com`. Merchants can disable them with the `advancedFraudSignals` parameter (or `setLoadParameters` in the module), which increases fraud risk such as card testing. Stripe states these signals are used only for security and anti-fraud and are not shared or sold to advertisers.

**Configurations included.**

| Configuration | Summary |
|---|---|
| Configuration A — Lower risk | Stripe.js loaded only on checkout; advanced fraud signals disabled. |
| Configuration B — Medium risk | Stripe.js loaded only on checkout; advanced fraud signals enabled. |
| Configuration C — Higher risk (default configuration) | Stripe.js loaded on every page with advanced fraud signals enabled. |

> ⚠ **Recommendation.** Fraud-prevention signals during a payment the visitor requested may be strictly necessary; collection on pages without a payment flow (C) is harder to justify. Have your DPO confirm whether B needs a consent entry and whether C requires consent.

## Step 1 — Configure Stripe

| # | Configuration area | Where to configure it | Configuration A — Lower risk | Configuration B — Medium risk | Configuration C — Higher risk (default configuration) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Processing purposes | Operator decision | Unlock additional website features | Unlock additional website features | Unlock additional website features |
| 2 | Loading scope | Theme / front-end | Checkout pages only | Checkout pages only | All pages |
| 3 | Advanced fraud signals | `advancedFraudSignals` / `setLoadParameters` | Disabled | Enabled | Enabled |

## Step 2 — Map the configuration in the Consenter Manager

**Processing location.** Stripe is a US enterprise; list the United States.
