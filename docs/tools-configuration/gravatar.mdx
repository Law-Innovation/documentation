<Callout type="info" title="Overview">
This guide explains how to implement different privacy configurations for Gravatar avatars and how to reflect them in the Consenter Manager.

1. Configure the third-party service.
2. Map the selected configuration in the Consenter Manager.
3. Update your privacy policy to reflect the actual implementation.

The configurations are comparative examples, not guarantees of legal compliance. Verify that the guide matches your product edition, implementation, and current provider documentation.
</Callout>

## At a glance

**Scope.** Gravatar avatars (Automattic Inc., US), typically in WordPress comments. Sources were checked on 16 November 2026.

**What the service does in this scope.** WordPress checks for a Gravatar by sending an MD5 hash of each displayed author's email address to gravatar.com; even the default avatars are served from gravatar.com [S1]. When a visitor opens the page, gravatar.com receives the visitor's IP address and the hashed email addresses [S2]. WordPress' suggested privacy text notes that the hash may be provided to Gravatar [S3]. Avatars can be disabled with `add_filter( 'get_avatar', '__return_empty_string', 1000 );` [S2].

**Configurations included.**

| Configuration | Summary |
|---|---|
| Configuration A — Lower risk | Gravatar disabled; local avatars or none. |
| Configuration B — Medium risk | Gravatar enabled; avatars loaded from gravatar.com. |

> ⚠ **Recommendation — Configuration B.** Avatar images are plain image requests, but they disclose the visitor's IP address and commenters' email hashes to a US provider [S1][S2]. Whether this needs consent or can rely on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR is debated in the WordPress community [S4]; have your DPO decide, or load comments with avatars after Consenter consent.

## Step 1 — Configure Gravatar

| # | Configuration area | Where to configure it | Configuration A — Lower risk | Configuration B — Medium risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Processing purposes | Operator decision | — | Unlock additional website features |
| 2 | Avatars | **Settings > Discussion > Avatars** or `get_avatar` filter [S2] | Off / local | Gravatar |
| 3 | Loading | Consenter (see ⚠) | — | Per DPO decision |

## Step 2 — Map the configuration in the Consenter Manager

| Consenter Manager field | Configuration A — Lower risk | Configuration B — Medium risk |
|---|---|---|
| Entry needed | No | Yes |
| Processing purposes | — | Unlock additional website features |
| Tracking method | No tracking | Third party tracking (single session, cross-website) |
| Identifier | No identifier | IP address, Probabilistic identifiers (email hash) |
| Data categories | — | IP address, Device characteristics, Probabilistic identifiers |
| Legal role of data recipient | — | Individual Controller |
| Personalisation model | — | No personalisation |
| Maximum storage duration | — | Per Automattic privacy policy |
| Processing location | — | United States (Automattic; 18 U.S.C. § 2713 [S5]) |
