Typical processing purposes: Unlock additional website features

<Callout type="info" title="Overview">
This guide explains how to implement different privacy configurations for Yumpu publications on a website and how to reflect them in the Consenter Manager.

1. Configure the third-party service.
2. Map the selected configuration in the Consenter Manager.
3. Update your privacy policy to reflect the actual implementation.

The configurations are comparative examples, not guarantees of legal compliance. Verify that the guide matches your product edition, implementation, and current provider documentation.
</Callout>

## At a glance

**Scope.** Yumpu flip-book publications (i-Magazine AG, Switzerland) linked or embedded.

**What the service does in this scope.** A website operator documents that the Yumpu embed sets a broad range of cookies, including Yumpu's own (`ypsession`, `yumpu_slc`) and cookies associated with Google Analytics and Ads, Meta, Microsoft Clarity and Bing, LinkedIn and others, with retention up to 2 years. Yumpu's privacy policy is written under the GDPR and the Swiss DSG.

**Configurations included.**

| Configuration | Summary |
|---|---|
| Configuration A — Lower risk | Link to the publication on Yumpu; no embed. |
| Configuration B — Higher risk | Embedded Yumpu reader loaded after Consenter consent / click-to-load. |

## Step 1 — Configure Yumpu

| # | Configuration area | Where to configure it | Configuration A — Lower risk | Configuration B — Higher risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Processing purposes | Operator decision | — | Unlock additional website features |
| 2 | Integration | Website code | Link | Yumpu embed code |
| 3 | Loading | Consenter script blocking / click-to-load | — | After consent |

## Step 2 — Map the configuration in the Consenter Manager

**Processing location.** Switzerland (adequacy decision); the embed loads US advertising and analytics vendors, subject to the US CLOUD Act; list the United States.

| Consenter Manager field | Configuration A — Lower risk | Configuration B — Higher risk |
|---|---|---|
| Entry needed | No | Yes |
| Processing purposes | — | Unlock additional website features |
| Tracking method | No tracking | Third party tracking (crosssession, cross-website) |
| Identifier | No identifier | Device identifiers |
| Data categories | — | Browsing and interaction data, Device characteristics, Device identifiers, IP address, Users' profiles |
| Legal role of data recipient | — | Individual Controller |
| Personalisation model | — | Profile based (embedded ad vendors) |
| Maximum storage duration | — | Up to 2 years |
| Processing location | — | Switzerland; United States |
