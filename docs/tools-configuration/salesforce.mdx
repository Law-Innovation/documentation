<Callout type="info" title="Overview">
This guide explains how to implement different privacy configurations for Account Engagement (formerly Pardot) and how to reflect them in the Consenter Manager.

1. Configure the third-party service.
2. Map the selected configuration in the Consenter Manager.
3. Update your privacy policy to reflect the actual implementation.

The configurations are comparative examples, not guarantees of legal compliance. Verify that the guide matches your product edition, implementation, and current provider documentation.
</Callout>

## At a glance

**Scope.** The Account Engagement website tracking code and Tracking & Consent JavaScript API. Salesforce, Inc. is a US company.

**What the service does in this scope.** Account Engagement uses two cookies, `visitor_id` (with the account ID appended) and `pi_opt_in`. With first-party tracking, tracking moves from the Pardot domain to the customer's tracker domain; it was enabled by default for new business units from February 2023. Pardot sets first-party cookies for tracking and third-party cookies for redundancy. With the Tracking & Consent API (activated in **Settings > Edit > Activate the Tracking & Consent JavaScript API**), `sendPageView` makes no request if `pi_opt_in` is false. When a visitor converts (e.g. by form), activity is associated with a prospect record.

**Configurations included.**

| Configuration | Summary |
|---|---|
| Configuration A — Medium risk | First-party tracking only; anonymous visitor analytics; after consent. |
| Configuration B — Higher risk (default configuration) | Tracking with third-party redundancy cookies; prospects identified via forms and email links; after consent. |

**No configuration without prior consent.** Both cookies are persistent visitor identifiers.

## Step 1 — Configure Account Engagement

| # | Configuration area | Where to configure it | Configuration A — Medium risk | Configuration B — Higher risk (default configuration) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Processing purposes | Operator decision | Support marketing analytics | Support marketing analytics; Receive personalised marketing offers |
| 2 | Tracking domain | **Settings > First-Party Tracking**; **Domain Management** | First-party tracker domain only | First-party with third-party redundancy |
| 3 | Consent | Tracking & Consent API; `pi_opt_in` | Set after Consenter consent | Set after Consenter consent |
| 4 | Prospect identification | Forms, email link tracking | Not used | Used |

## Step 2 — Map the configuration in the Consenter Manager

**Processing location.** Salesforce is a US enterprise subject to the US CLOUD Act; list the United States in all configurations, in addition to the hosting region in your contract.

| Consenter Manager field | Configuration A — Medium risk | Configuration B — Higher risk (default configuration) |
|---|---|---|
| Processing purposes | Support marketing analytics | Support marketing analytics, Receive personalised marketing offers |
| Tracking method | First party tracking (cross-session) | Third party tracking (crosssession, cross-website) |
| Identifier | Device identifiers | Device identifiers, Direct identifier |
| Data categories | Browsing and interaction data, Device characteristics, Device identifiers, IP address, Privacy choices | As A, plus direct identifier, User-provided data, Users' profiles |
| Legal role of data recipient | Processor | Processor |
| Personalisation model | No personalisation | Profile based |
| Maximum storage duration | Per cookie settings | Per cookie settings |
| Processing location | Contract region; United States | Contract region; United States |
