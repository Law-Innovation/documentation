Typical processing purposes: Support marketing analytics, Receive personalised marketing offers

> This guide explains how to implement different privacy configurations for Account Engagement (formerly Pardot) and how to reflect them in the Consenter Manager.
>
> 1. Configure the third-party service.
> 2. Map the selected configuration in the Consenter Manager.
> 3. Update your privacy policy to reflect the actual implementation.
>
> The configurations are comparative examples, not guarantees of legal compliance. Verify that the guide matches your product edition, implementation, and current provider documentation.

## At a glance

**Scope.** The Account Engagement website tracking code and Tracking & Consent JavaScript API. Salesforce, Inc. is a US company. Sources were checked on 16 November 2026.

**What the service does in this scope.** Account Engagement uses two cookies, `visitor_id` (with the account ID appended) and `pi_opt_in` [S1]. With first-party tracking, tracking moves from the Pardot domain to the customer's tracker domain [S2]; it was enabled by default for new business units from February 2023 [S3]. Pardot sets first-party cookies for tracking and third-party cookies for redundancy [S4]. With the Tracking & Consent API (activated in **Settings > Edit > Activate the Tracking & Consent JavaScript API**), `sendPageView` makes no request if `pi_opt_in` is false [S5][S6]. When a visitor converts (e.g. by form), activity is associated with a prospect record [S1].

**Configurations included.**

| Configuration | Summary |
|---|---|
| Configuration A — Medium risk | First-party tracking only; anonymous visitor analytics; after consent. |
| Configuration B — Higher risk (default behaviour) | Tracking with third-party redundancy cookies; prospects identified via forms and email links; after consent. |

**No configuration without prior consent.** Both cookies are persistent visitor identifiers [S1].

## Step 1 — Configure Account Engagement

| # | Configuration area | Where to configure it | Configuration A — Medium risk | Configuration B — Higher risk (default behaviour) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Processing purposes | Operator decision | Support marketing analytics | Support marketing analytics; Receive personalised marketing offers |
| 2 | Tracking domain | **Settings > First-Party Tracking**; **Domain Management** [S2][S3] | First-party tracker domain only | First-party with third-party redundancy |
| 3 | Consent | Tracking & Consent API; `pi_opt_in` [S5][S6] | Set after Consenter consent | Set after Consenter consent |
| 4 | Prospect identification | Forms, email link tracking | Not used | Used |

## Step 2 — Map the configuration in the Consenter Manager

**Processing location.** Salesforce is a US enterprise subject to 18 U.S.C. § 2713 [S7]; list the United States in all configurations, in addition to the hosting region in your contract.

| Consenter Manager field | Configuration A — Medium risk | Configuration B — Higher risk (default behaviour) |
|---|---|---|
| Processing purposes | Support marketing analytics | Support marketing analytics, Receive personalised marketing offers |
| Tracking method | First party tracking (cross-session) | Third party tracking (crosssession, cross-website) |
| Identifier | Device identifiers | Device identifiers, Direct identifier |
| Data categories | Browsing and interaction data, Device characteristics, Device identifiers, IP address, Privacy choices | As A, plus direct identifier, User-provided data, Users' profiles |
| Legal role of data recipient | Processor | Processor |
| Personalisation model | No personalisation | Profile based |
| Maximum storage duration | Per cookie settings | Per cookie settings |
| Processing location | Contract region; United States | Contract region; United States |

**Basis.** Cookies [S1]; third-party redundancy cookies [S4]; `pi_opt_in` [S6], hence "Privacy choices".
