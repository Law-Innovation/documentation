<Callout type="info" title="Overview">
This guide explains how to implement different privacy configurations for the Freshchat web messenger and how to reflect them in the Consenter Manager.

1. Configure the third-party service.
2. Map the selected configuration in the Consenter Manager.
3. Update your privacy policy to reflect the actual implementation.

The configurations are comparative examples, not guarantees of legal compliance. Verify that the guide matches your product edition, implementation, and current provider documentation.
</Callout>

## At a glance

**Scope.** The Freshchat web messenger (conversations widget) and self-service widget. Freshworks Inc. is a US company.

**What the service does in this scope.** Freshworks recommends not loading the web messenger or self-service widget until the visitor has accepted the cookie policy, and using `window.fcWidget.init({})` so the website decides when to load the widget. Freshchat offers EU data-centre hosting for EU customers and a DPA. Freshworks does not publish a Freshchat-specific cookie list in the sources; verify the `fc_*` cookies in the browser.

**Configurations included.**

| Configuration | Summary |
|---|---|
| Configuration A — Medium risk | Widget initialised after consent; anonymous visitors; EU data centre. |
| Configuration B — Higher risk | Widget initialised after consent; logged-in users identified (external ID, email); events timeline on. |

## Step 1 — Configure Freshchat

| # | Configuration area | Where to configure it | Configuration A — Medium risk | Configuration B — Higher risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Processing purposes | Operator decision | Unlock additional website features | Unlock additional website features |
| 2 | Loading | `window.fcWidget.init({})` after Consenter consent | After consent | After consent |
| 3 | Data centre | Account region | EU | EU or US |
| 4 | User identification | `externalId`, email in `init` | Not passed | Passed |
| 5 | Events timeline | Freshchat settings | Off | On |

## Step 2 — Map the configuration in the Consenter Manager

**Processing location.** Freshworks is a US enterprise subject to the US CLOUD Act; list the United States in all configurations.

| Consenter Manager field | Configuration A — Medium risk | Configuration B — Higher risk |
|---|---|---|
| Processing purposes | Unlock additional website features | Unlock additional website features |
| Tracking method | First party tracking (cross-session) | First party tracking (cross-session) |
| Identifier | Device identifiers | Device identifiers, Authentication-derived identifiers, Direct identifier |
| Data categories | Browsing and interaction data, Device characteristics, Device identifiers, User-provided data | As A, plus Authentication-derived identifiers, direct identifier |
| Legal role of data recipient | Processor | Processor |
| Personalisation model | No personalisation | No personalisation |
| Maximum storage duration | Per cookie inspection | Per cookie inspection |
| Processing location | EU; United States | EU or United States; United States |
