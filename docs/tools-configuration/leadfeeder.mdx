<Callout type="info" title="Overview">
This guide explains how to implement different privacy configurations for the Leadfeeder Tracker and how to reflect them in the Consenter Manager.

1. Configure the third-party service.
2. Map the selected configuration in the Consenter Manager.
3. Update your privacy policy to reflect the actual implementation.

The configurations are comparative examples, not guarantees of legal compliance. Verify that the guide matches your product edition, implementation, and current provider documentation.
</Callout>

## At a glance

**Scope.** The Leadfeeder Tracker (Dealfront) for B2B company identification on a website. Sources were checked on 16 November 2026.

**What the service does in this scope.** The tracker analyses technical signals such as the IP address, matches them against company databases and enriches the visit with firmographic details; the script is served via AWS CloudFront [S1]. It can set the `_lfa` cookie (1 year) as a visitor ID [S2]. With consent-manager support, the tracker sets cookies only after the CMP calls `window.ldfdr.acceptCookie()` [S3]; until then, no cookie is dropped [S4]. **IP anonymisation** stores only a shortened IP address and keeps the actual IP out of all systems including logs [S4][S5]. Dealfront states that it processes website traffic data on its own legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) in optimising its products, services, sales and marketing [S4].

**Configurations included.**

| Configuration | Summary |
|---|---|
| Configuration A — Medium risk (⚠ no-consent) | Cookieless company identification; IP anonymisation on; cookie only after consent. |
| Configuration B — Higher risk | Tracker with `_lfa` visitor cookie after consent; IP anonymisation off; CRM integration. |

> ⚠ **Recommendation — running the tracker before consent is legally uncertain.** Dealfront positions company-level identification on legitimate interest [S1][S4]. The script still runs in the visitor's browser and processes the IP address, which can be personal data; Dealfront itself notes that a balancing test is needed [S6]. Have your DPO or independent counsel confirm before loading the tracker without consent.

## Step 1 — Configure Leadfeeder

| # | Configuration area | Where to configure it | Configuration A — Medium risk | Configuration B — Higher risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Processing purposes | Operator decision | Support marketing analytics | Support marketing analytics; Receive marketing offers |
| 2 | Cookie consent integration | Tracker settings: consent-manager support; CMP calls `window.ldfdr.acceptCookie()` [S3] | Cookie only after consent | Cookie only after consent |
| 3 | IP anonymisation | Tracker settings [S5] | On | Off |
| 4 | Tracker loading | Consenter / tag manager | See ⚠ recommendation | After consent |
| 5 | CRM integrations | Dealfront integrations (e.g. HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive) | Not used | Used |

## Step 2 — Map the configuration in the Consenter Manager

**Processing location.** Dealfront is an EU group; the script is delivered via AWS CloudFront [S1]. Amazon Web Services is a US enterprise subject to 18 U.S.C. § 2713 [S7]; list the United States as a potential access location.

| Consenter Manager field | Configuration A — Medium risk | Configuration B — Higher risk |
|---|---|---|
| Processing purposes | Support marketing analytics | Support marketing analytics, Receive marketing offers |
| Tracking method | No tracking | First party tracking (cross-session) |
| Identifier | IP address | Device identifiers, IP address |
| Data categories | IP address anonymised, Browsing and interaction data, Device characteristics | IP address, Device identifiers, Browsing and interaction data, Device characteristics, Users' profiles |
| Legal role of data recipient | Processor; Dealfront also Individual Controller for its own product purposes [S4] | Same |
| Personalisation model | No personalisation | Group based (properties) |
| Maximum storage duration | Not documented | `_lfa` 1 year [S2] |
| Processing location | EU; United States as potential access location | Same |

**Basis.** IP processing and enrichment [S1]; cookie [S2]; IP anonymisation [S5]; Dealfront's own legitimate-interest processing [S4]. "Group based (properties)" in B reflects company-level (firmographic) segmentation for sales outreach. Because Dealfront also processes data for its own purposes, consider a second entry with "Individual Controller".

## Privacy-policy follow-up

- Dealfront (Leadfeeder) as recipient and, for its own purposes, controller [S4]; IP-based company identification; cookies (B); opt-out; transfers.
