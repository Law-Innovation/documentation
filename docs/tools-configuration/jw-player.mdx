<Callout type="info" title="Overview">
This guide explains how to implement different privacy configurations for JW Player and how to reflect them in the Consenter Manager.

1. Configure the third-party service.
2. Map the selected configuration in the Consenter Manager.
3. Update your privacy policy to reflect the actual implementation.

The configurations are comparative examples, not guarantees of legal compliance. Verify that the guide matches your product edition, implementation, and current provider documentation.
</Callout>

## At a glance

**Scope.** The JW Player web player with optional advertising (JWP, Inc., New York, United States [S1][S2]). Sources were checked on 16 November 2026.

**What the service does in this scope.** The `doNotSaveCookies` setup property permits or prohibits the player from storing user IDs; by default (`false`) user cookies are stored, and JW Player suggests `true` when a user has opted out via a CMP [S3]. JW Player supports IAB TCF v2 in VAST and Google IMA advertising [S4].

> **Note on consent signals.** Advertising in the player expects a TCF consent string [S4]. Consenter does not currently support TCF communication, so this guide does not cover configuring the signal; load the player only after consent in Consenter.

**Configurations included.**

| Configuration | Summary |
|---|---|
| Configuration A — Medium risk | Player without ads; `doNotSaveCookies: true`; player after consent. |
| Configuration B — Higher risk (default configuration) | Player with user cookies and VAST / Google IMA ads; after consent. |

**No configuration without prior consent.** Even with `doNotSaveCookies: true`, the player loads from JW Player's CDN and sends analytics; the sources do not establish that this is free of device access.

## Step 1 — Configure JW Player

| # | Configuration area | Where to configure it | Configuration A — Medium risk | Configuration B — Higher risk (default configuration) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Processing purposes | Operator decision | Unlock additional website features | Unlock additional website features; Customise online ads |
| 2 | `doNotSaveCookies` | `jwplayer().setup()` [S3] | `true` | `false` (default) |
| 3 | Advertising | VAST / Google IMA [S4] | Off | On |
| 4 | Loading | Consenter script blocking | After consent | After consent |

## Step 2 — Map the configuration in the Consenter Manager

**Processing location.** JWP is a US enterprise subject to 18 U.S.C. § 2713 [S5]; list the United States. Ad partners (e.g. Google) need their own entries as independent controllers.

| Consenter Manager field | Configuration A — Medium risk | Configuration B — Higher risk (default configuration) |
|---|---|---|
| Processing purposes | Unlock additional website features | Unlock additional website features, Customise online ads |
| Tracking method | Third party tracking (single session, cross-website) | Third party tracking (crosssession, cross-website) |
| Identifier | No identifier | Device identifiers |
| Data categories | Browsing and interaction data, Device characteristics, IP address | Browsing and interaction data, Device characteristics, Device identifiers, IP address, Users' profiles |
| Legal role of data recipient | Processor | Processor (JW Player); ad partners as Individual Controller |
| Personalisation model | No personalisation | Profile based |
| Maximum storage duration | Session | Per cookie inspection |
| Processing location | United States | United States |
