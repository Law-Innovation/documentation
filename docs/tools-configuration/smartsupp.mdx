Typical processing purposes: Unlock additional website features, Support marketing analytics

> This guide explains how to implement different privacy configurations for the Smartsupp chat box and how to reflect them in the Consenter Manager.
>
> 1. Configure the third-party service.
> 2. Map the selected configuration in the Consenter Manager.
> 3. Update your privacy policy to reflect the actual implementation.
>
> The configurations are comparative examples, not guarantees of legal compliance. Verify that the guide matches your product edition, implementation, and current provider documentation.

## At a glance

**Scope.** The Smartsupp chat box (Smartsupp.com, s.r.o., Brno, Czech Republic). Smartsupp states that its data is stored only within the EU [S1]. Sources were checked on 16 November 2026.

**What the service does in this scope.** `ssupp.vid` identifies visitors so that a conversation can be paired with the visitor on reload; `ssupp.visits` and its local-storage equivalent are marketing information that should not be stored without marketing consent [S2]. Cookie consent is enabled in **Settings → Security and privacy → Cookie consent**; when enabled, only necessary cookies are stored, and analytics and marketing cookies are stored only after `smartsupp('analyticsConsent', true)` / `smartsupp('marketingConsent', true)` [S3][S4]. Smartsupp is the processor under its DPA [S5].

**Configurations included.**

| Configuration | Summary |
|---|---|
| Configuration A — Medium risk | Cookie consent mode on; chat box after Consenter consent; analytics and marketing consent not passed. |
| Configuration B — Higher risk | Cookie consent mode on; analytics and marketing consent passed from Consenter; visitor recordings used. |

> ⚠ **Recommendation — chat box before consent.** Smartsupp states that no consent is needed for basic Flow (formerly chatbots) only [S2], and its consent mode still stores `ssupp.vid` before consent [S3][S4]. Load the chat box after Consenter consent unless your DPO or independent counsel confirms `ssupp.vid` is strictly necessary.

## Step 1 — Configure Smartsupp

| # | Configuration area | Where to configure it | Configuration A — Medium risk | Configuration B — Higher risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Processing purposes | Operator decision | Unlock additional website features | Unlock additional website features; Support marketing analytics |
| 2 | Cookie consent mode | **Settings → Security and privacy → Cookie consent** [S3] | On | On |
| 3 | Consent signals | `smartsupp('analyticsConsent', …)`, `smartsupp('marketingConsent', …)` [S4] | `false` | Mapped from Consenter |
| 4 | Visitor recordings | Smartsupp recordings | Off | On |
| 5 | Loading | Consenter script blocking | After consent | After consent |

## Step 2 — Map the configuration in the Consenter Manager

| Consenter Manager field | Configuration A — Medium risk | Configuration B — Higher risk |
|---|---|---|
| Processing purposes | Unlock additional website features | Unlock additional website features, Support marketing analytics |
| Tracking method | First party tracking (cross-session) | First party tracking (cross-session) |
| Identifier | Device identifiers | Device identifiers |
| Data categories | Browsing and interaction data, Device identifiers, User-provided data | As A, plus Device characteristics, Privacy choices, Users' profiles |
| Legal role of data recipient | Processor | Processor |
| Personalisation model | No personalisation | Group based (behaviour) |
| Maximum storage duration | Until deleted (persistent storage) [S2] | Until deleted [S2] |
| Processing location | EU [S1] | EU [S1]; check the DPA for the recordings sub-processor |

**Basis.** Cookies [S2]; consent signals [S4], hence "Privacy choices" in B. Smartsupp is a Czech company; no CLOUD Act flag unless the DPA lists US sub-processors.
