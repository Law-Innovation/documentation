<Callout type="info" title="Overview">
This guide explains how to implement different privacy configurations for Twitch content on a website and how to reflect them in the Consenter Manager.

1. Configure the third-party service.
2. Map the selected configuration in the Consenter Manager.
3. Update your privacy policy to reflect the actual implementation.

The configurations are comparative examples, not guarantees of legal compliance. Verify that the guide matches your product edition, implementation, and current provider documentation.
</Callout>

## At a glance

**Scope.** Twitch embedded video, clips and chat (Twitch Interactive, Inc., an Amazon company, US). Sources were checked on 16 November 2026.

**What the service does in this scope.** Twitch embeds require a `parent` parameter naming the embedding domain; without it, playback shows an error directing viewers to Twitch [S1]. Twitch confirmed that the embedded player serves ads [S2]. Twitch's cookie notice describes the cookies it stores on devices [S3].

**Configurations included.**

| Configuration | Summary |
|---|---|
| Configuration A — Lower risk | Link to the Twitch channel; no embed. |
| Configuration B — Higher risk | Embedded player (with ads) loaded after Consenter consent. |

## Step 1 — Configure Twitch

| # | Configuration area | Where to configure it | Configuration A — Lower risk | Configuration B — Higher risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Processing purposes | Operator decision | — | Unlock additional website features; Customise online ads |
| 2 | Integration | Website code [S1] | Link | `player.twitch.tv` iframe or JS embed with `parent` |
| 3 | Loading | Consenter script blocking / click-to-load | — | After consent |

## Step 2 — Map the configuration in the Consenter Manager

**Processing location.** Twitch is a US enterprise subject to 18 U.S.C. § 2713 [S4]; list the United States.

| Consenter Manager field | Configuration A — Lower risk | Configuration B — Higher risk |
|---|---|---|
| Entry needed | No | Yes |
| Processing purposes | — | Unlock additional website features, Customise online ads |
| Tracking method | No tracking | Third party tracking (crosssession, cross-website) |
| Identifier | No identifier | Device identifiers, Authentication-derived identifiers (logged-in viewers) |
| Data categories | — | Browsing and interaction data, Device characteristics, Device identifiers, IP address, Users' profiles |
| Legal role of data recipient | — | Individual Controller |
| Personalisation model | — | Profile based |
| Maximum storage duration | — | Per Twitch cookie notice [S3] |
| Processing location | — | United States |
