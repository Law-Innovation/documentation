Typical processing purposes: Improve the service

<Callout type="info" title="Overview">
This guide explains how to implement different privacy configurations for Yandex Metrica and how to reflect them in the Consenter Manager. Yandex Direct (advertising) is not covered; the research ran out before its documentation could be checked.

1. Configure the third-party service.
2. Map the selected configuration in the Consenter Manager.
3. Update your privacy policy to reflect the actual implementation.

The configurations are comparative examples, not guarantees of legal compliance. Verify that the guide matches your product edition, implementation, and current provider documentation.
</Callout>

## At a glance

**Scope.** Yandex Metrica tag on the website. Sources were checked on 16 November 2026.

**What the service does in this scope.** To count users, Metrica uses anonymous browser identifiers stored in cookies or `localStorage` on the top-level domain [S1]. `_ym_uid` identifies site users for 1 year; `_ym_visorc_*` (30 minutes) allows Session Replay to work; `gdpr` and `is_gdpr` identify users under GDPR jurisdiction [S1]. Metrica also sets identifiers on Yandex domains such as `yandexuid` and `ymex` (1 year) [S2].

**Configurations included.**

| Configuration | Summary |
|---|---|
| Configuration A — Higher risk | Metrica after Consenter consent; Session Replay off. |
| Configuration B — Higher risk | Metrica after consent with Session Replay on. |

## Step 1 — Configure Yandex Metrica

| # | Configuration area | Where to configure it | Configuration A — Higher risk | Configuration B — Higher risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Processing purposes | Operator decision | Improve the service | Improve the service |
| 2 | Loading | Tag manager / Consenter | After consent | After consent |
| 3 | Session Replay | Metrica tag settings [S1] | Off | On |

## Step 2 — Map the configuration in the Consenter Manager

**Processing location.** Yandex is a Russian company; list the Russian Federation as a third country and check Yandex's terms for the contracting entity.

| Consenter Manager field | Configuration A — Higher risk | Configuration B — Higher risk |
|---|---|---|
| Processing purposes | Improve the service | Improve the service |
| Tracking method | Third party tracking (crosssession, cross-website) | Third party tracking (crosssession, cross-website) |
| Identifier | Device identifiers | Device identifiers |
| Data categories | Aggregated site statistics, Browsing and interaction data, Device identifiers, Device characteristics | Aggregated site statistics, Browsing and interaction data, Device identifiers, Device characteristics, User-provided data |
| Legal role of data recipient | Processor | Processor |
| Personalisation model | No personalisation | No personalisation |
| Maximum storage duration | 1 year (`_ym_uid`) [S1] | 1 year (`_ym_uid`) [S1] |
| Processing location | Russian Federation | Russian Federation |

> **Note on legal role and tracking method.** Yandex-domain identifiers (`yandexuid`, `ymex`) are set across Yandex properties [S2], which is why the table uses third-party tracking. Confirm Yandex's role (processor or controller) in its Metrica terms.
