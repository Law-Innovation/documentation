Typical processing purposes: Unlock additional website features

<Callout type="info" title="Overview">
This guide explains how to implement different privacy configurations for the Amazon Pay button on a merchant website and how to reflect them in the Consenter Manager. Processing a payment the visitor has chosen is out of scope.

1. Configure the third-party service.
2. Map the selected configuration in the Consenter Manager.
3. Update your privacy policy to reflect the actual implementation.

The configurations are comparative examples, not guarantees of legal compliance. Verify that the guide matches your product edition, implementation, and current provider documentation.
</Callout>

## At a glance

**Scope.** Amazon Pay Checkout v2 (`checkout.js`). Sources were checked on 16 November 2026.

**What the service does in this scope.** The `checkout.js` script renders the Amazon Pay button; Amazon recommends placing the Express button wherever checkout starts, such as the mini cart, cart page or checkout page, or in the payment selection at the end of checkout [S1]. Amazon Payments uses cookies to recognise the browser or device [S2]. Shop modules document loading all Amazon Pay scripts only after consent through a CMP [S3].

**Configurations included.**

| Configuration | Summary |
|---|---|
| Configuration A — Lower risk | Amazon Pay offered only in the payment selection at the end of checkout; script loaded when selected. |
| Configuration B — Medium risk | Express button on mini cart / cart pages; script loaded after Consenter consent. |

> ⚠ **Recommendation.** Whether loading `checkout.js` in A is strictly necessary for the payment the visitor requested is a legal assessment. Have your DPO confirm it before running A without a consent entry.

## Step 1 — Configure Amazon Pay

| # | Configuration area | Where to configure it | Configuration A — Lower risk | Configuration B — Medium risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Processing purposes | Operator decision | Unlock additional website features | Unlock additional website features |
| 2 | Button placement | Shop integration [S1] | Payment selection at end of checkout | Mini cart / cart / product pages |
| 3 | Loading | Shop template / CMP [S3] | On selection | After Consenter consent |

## Step 2 — Map the configuration in the Consenter Manager

**Processing location.** Amazon Payments Europe operates from Luxembourg as part of a US group; list the United States (18 U.S.C. § 2713 [S4]).

| Consenter Manager field | Configuration A — Lower risk | Configuration B — Medium risk |
|---|---|---|
| Entry needed | Per DPO assessment (⚠) | Yes |
| Processing purposes | Unlock additional website features | Unlock additional website features |
| Tracking method | Third party tracking (crosssession, cross-website) | Third party tracking (crosssession, cross-website) |
| Identifier | Device identifiers, Authentication-derived identifiers | Device identifiers, Authentication-derived identifiers |
| Data categories | Device identifiers, Device characteristics, Authentication-derived identifiers, e-commerce Activity | Device identifiers, Device characteristics, Authentication-derived identifiers, Browsing and interaction data, e-commerce Activity |
| Legal role of data recipient | Individual Controller | Individual Controller |
| Personalisation model | No personalisation | No personalisation |
| Maximum storage duration | Per Amazon Payments cookie notice | Per Amazon Payments cookie notice |
| Processing location | EU (Luxembourg) / United States | EU (Luxembourg) / United States |
