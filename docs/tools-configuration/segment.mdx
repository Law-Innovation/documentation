Typical processing purposes: Improve the service, Support marketing analytics

<Callout type="info" title="Overview">
This guide explains how to implement different privacy configurations for Twilio Segment and how to reflect them in the Consenter Manager.

1. Configure the third-party service.
2. Map the selected configuration in the Consenter Manager.
3. Update your privacy policy to reflect the actual implementation.

The configurations are comparative examples, not guarantees of legal compliance. Verify that the guide matches your product edition, implementation, and current provider documentation.
</Callout>

## At a glance

**Scope.** Segment Analytics.js on a website as a customer data platform that collects events and forwards them to destinations. Each **destination** (for example an analytics or ad tool) is a separate recipient and must be assessed and entered separately. Twilio Inc. is a US company. Sources were checked on 16 November 2026.

**What the service does in this scope.** Analytics.js sets first-party cookies `ajs_anonymous_id` (anonymous visitor), `ajs_user_id` (logged-in user, after `identify()`) and `ajs_group_id` (after `group()`) as soon as the site loads [S1]; IDs are also persisted in local storage [S2]. Segment's Consent Management (Business tier) enforces consent across destinations [S3]. EU data residency requires an EU workspace and the EU endpoint `events.eu1.segmentapis.com`; only EU workspaces store data exclusively in the EU [S4].

**Configurations included.**

| Configuration | Summary |
|---|---|
| Configuration A — Medium risk | EU workspace; Analytics.js loads after consent; no `identify()`; cloud-mode destinations limited to analytics. |
| Configuration B — Higher risk | US workspace; `identify()` with traits (email); device-mode and advertising destinations; after consent. |

**No configuration without prior consent.** `ajs_anonymous_id` is set on load [S1]; no consent-free configuration is documented.

## Step 1 — Configure Segment

| # | Configuration area | Where to configure it | Configuration A — Medium risk | Configuration B — Higher risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Processing purposes | Operator decision | Improve the service | Improve the service; Support marketing analytics; Receive personalised marketing offers (if ad destinations) |
| 2 | Workspace region | Create EU workspace or request migration; `apiHost: "events.eu1.segmentapis.com/v1"` [S4] | EU | US |
| 3 | Loading | Consenter blocks Analytics.js until consent | After consent | After consent |
| 4 | Consent Management | Segment Consent Management / consent wrapper [S3] | Map destinations to consent categories | Map destinations to consent categories |
| 5 | Identify / traits | `analytics.identify(userId, traits)` | Not used | Used |
| 6 | Destinations | Connections > Destinations | Cloud-mode analytics only | Device-mode and advertising destinations |
| 7 | Persistence | `user.persist`, cookie/localStorage keys [S2] | Default | Default |

## Step 2 — Map the configuration in the Consenter Manager

**Processing location.** Twilio Inc. is a US company and can be required to disclose data in its possession, custody or control regardless of storage location (18 U.S.C. § 2713) [S5].

| Consenter Manager field | Configuration A — Medium risk | Configuration B — Higher risk |
|---|---|---|
| Processing purposes | Improve the service | Improve the service, Support marketing analytics |
| Tracking method | First party tracking (cross-session) | First party tracking (cross-session) |
| Identifier | Device identifiers, IP address | Device identifiers, IP address, Authentication-derived identifiers, Direct identifier |
| Data categories | Browsing and interaction data, Device characteristics, Device identifiers, IP address | Browsing and interaction data, Device characteristics, Device identifiers, IP address, Authentication-derived identifiers, direct identifier, Users' profiles |
| Legal role of data recipient | Processor | Processor |
| Personalisation model | No personalisation | Profile based (if profiles feed personalised marketing) |
| Maximum storage duration | Per contract | Per contract |
| Processing location | EU workspace; United States as potential access location | United States |

**Basis.** Cookies and identity [S1][S2]; region [S4]. Every destination receiving data must have its own Consenter Manager entry.

## Privacy-policy follow-up

- Segment as processor; identifiers; destinations and their roles; region; potential access by US authorities [S5].

## Verification checklist

- [ ] No `cdn.segment.com` load and no `ajs_*` cookies before consent.
- [ ] Destinations receive data only for granted categories [S3].
