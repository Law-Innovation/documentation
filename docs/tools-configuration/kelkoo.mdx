<Callout type="info" title="Overview">
This guide explains how to implement different privacy configurations for Kelkoo Sales Tracking (KST) on a merchant website and how to reflect them in the Consenter Manager.

1. Configure the third-party service.
2. Map the selected configuration in the Consenter Manager.
3. Update your privacy policy to reflect the actual implementation.

The configurations are comparative examples, not guarantees of legal compliance. Verify that the guide matches your product edition, implementation, and current provider documentation.
</Callout>

## At a glance

**Scope.** Kelkoo Sales Tracking Lead Tag and Conversion Tag. Sources were checked on 16 November 2026.

**What the service does in this scope.** KST uses first-party cookies set on the merchant's own domain [S1]. The Lead Tag sets the tracking cookie when a user arrives from a Kelkoo Group ad; the Conversion Tag reads it on the confirmation page and sends order data [S2][S3]. The `kelkooId` cookie lasts 12 months [S1]. Kelkoo states that the Lead Tag must be gated behind consent while the Conversion Tag does not require separate consent, relying on legitimate interest for the order data it sends [S1][S4]. A server-to-server option exists, with consent managed directly by the merchant [S5].

**Configurations included.**

| Configuration | Summary |
|---|---|
| Configuration A — Medium risk | Lead Tag and Conversion Tag both loaded only after Consenter consent. |
| Configuration B — Medium risk (default behaviour) | Lead Tag after consent; Conversion Tag without consent, as Kelkoo recommends. |

> ⚠ **Recommendation.** In B the Conversion Tag reads a cookie only set after consent, but sends order data on the basis of legitimate interest. Have your DPO confirm this legal basis (GDPR) separately from the device-access question (§ 25 TDDDG).

## Step 1 — Configure Kelkoo Sales Tracking

| # | Configuration area | Where to configure it | Configuration A — Medium risk | Configuration B — Medium risk (default behaviour) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Processing purposes | Operator decision | Support marketing analytics | Support marketing analytics |
| 2 | Lead Tag | Landing pages / tag manager [S3] | After consent | After consent [S4] |
| 3 | Conversion Tag | Confirmation page [S3] | After consent | Without consent [S1] |
