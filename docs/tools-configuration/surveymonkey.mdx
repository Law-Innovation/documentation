Typical processing purposes: Improve the service

> This guide explains how to implement different privacy configurations for SurveyMonkey surveys on a website and how to reflect them in the Consenter Manager.
>
> 1. Configure the third-party service.
> 2. Map the selected configuration in the Consenter Manager.
> 3. Update your privacy policy to reflect the actual implementation.
>
> The configurations are comparative examples, not guarantees of legal compliance. Verify that the guide matches your product edition, implementation, and current provider documentation.

## At a glance

**Scope.** SurveyMonkey surveys linked from or embedded on the website. SurveyMonkey (SurveyMonkey Inc., US) generally provides services to EU, UK and Swiss customers through SurveyMonkey Europe UC (Ireland) [S1]. Sources were checked on 16 November 2026.

**What the service does in this scope.** SurveyMonkey has data centres in the US, Ireland and Canada; EU data hosting has been offered to new Enterprise customers [S1][S2]. SurveyMonkey relies on DPF certification and SCCs for transfers [S1]. Its cookie notice distinguishes required cookies from others used on its sites and services [S3].

**Configurations included.**

| Configuration | Summary |
|---|---|
| Configuration A — Medium risk | Link to the hosted survey; anonymous responses. |
| Configuration B — Higher risk | Embedded website survey or pop-up loaded after Consenter consent. |

## Step 1 — Configure SurveyMonkey

| # | Configuration area | Where to configure it | Configuration A — Medium risk | Configuration B — Higher risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Processing purposes | Operator decision | Improve the service | Improve the service |
| 2 | Integration | Collector type | Web link | Website embed / pop-up |
| 3 | Anonymous responses | Collector settings | On | Off |
| 4 | Data hosting | Contract [S2] | EU (Enterprise) | US |
| 5 | Loading | Consenter script blocking | — | After consent |

## Step 2 — Map the configuration in the Consenter Manager

**Processing location.** SurveyMonkey Inc. is a US enterprise subject to 18 U.S.C. § 2713 [S4]; list the United States.

| Consenter Manager field | Configuration A — Medium risk | Configuration B — Higher risk |
|---|---|---|
| Entry needed | No (mention in the privacy policy) | Yes |
| Processing purposes | — | Improve the service |
| Tracking method | No tracking | Third party tracking (crosssession, cross-website) |
| Identifier | No identifier | Device identifiers, IP address |
| Data categories | — | Browsing and interaction data, Device characteristics, Device identifiers, IP address, User-provided data |
| Legal role of data recipient | — | Processor |
| Personalisation model | — | No personalisation |
| Maximum storage duration | — | Per SurveyMonkey cookie notice [S3] |
| Processing location | — | Ireland / United States; United States |
