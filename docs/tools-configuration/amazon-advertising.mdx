Typical processing purposes: Support marketing analytics, Customise online ads

> This guide explains how to implement different privacy configurations for the Amazon Ad Tag on an advertiser website and how to reflect them in the Consenter Manager.
>
> 1. Configure the third-party service.
> 2. Map the selected configuration in the Consenter Manager.
> 3. Update your privacy policy to reflect the actual implementation.
>
> The configurations are comparative examples, not guarantees of legal compliance. Verify that the guide matches your product edition, implementation, and current provider documentation.

## At a glance

**Scope.** Amazon Ad Tag (`amzn.js`) for conversion measurement and audiences. Amazon Pay is covered separately. Sources were checked on 16 November 2026.

**What the service does in this scope.** Advertisers sending personal information to Amazon Ads must use either the IAB TCF signal or the Amazon Consent Signal (ACS) [S1]. ACS consists of `amzn_user_data` (consent to process personal data for advertising) and `amzn_ad_storage` (consent to read or write advertising cookies), each `GRANTED` or `DENIED` [S2]. Amazon provides `amzn-consent.js`; the Ad Tag listens for the consent event and restricts event triggers and first-party data collection accordingly [S3]. Amazon Ads is IAB TCF vendor 793 [S4].

**Configurations included.**

| Configuration | Summary |
|---|---|
| Configuration A — Higher risk | Ad Tag with ACS from Consenter: both signals `GRANTED` only after consent. |
| Configuration B — Higher risk | Ad Tag without a consent signal. |

## Step 1 — Configure the Amazon Ad Tag

| # | Configuration area | Where to configure it | Configuration A — Higher risk | Configuration B — Higher risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Processing purposes | Operator decision | Support marketing analytics; Customise online ads | Support marketing analytics; Customise online ads |
| 2 | Consent signal | `amzn-consent.js` (ACS) or TCF [S1][S3] | `amzn_user_data` / `amzn_ad_storage` from Consenter | None |

## Step 2 — Map the configuration in the Consenter Manager

**Processing location.** Amazon is a US enterprise; list the United States (18 U.S.C. § 2713 [S5]).

| Consenter Manager field | Configuration A — Higher risk | Configuration B — Higher risk |
|---|---|---|
| Processing purposes | Support marketing analytics, Customise online ads | Support marketing analytics, Customise online ads |
| Tracking method | Third party tracking (crosssession, cross-website) | Third party tracking (crosssession, cross-website) |
| Identifier | Device identifiers | Device identifiers |
| Data categories | Device identifiers, Browsing and interaction data, e-commerce Activity, Users' profiles, Privacy choices | Device identifiers, Browsing and interaction data, e-commerce Activity, Users' profiles |
| Legal role of data recipient | Individual Controller | Individual Controller |
| Personalisation model | Profile based | Profile based |
| Maximum storage duration | Per Amazon Ads terms | Per Amazon Ads terms |
| Processing location | EU / United States | EU / United States |
