Typical processing purposes: Support marketing analytics, Receive marketing offers

<Callout type="info" title="Overview">
This guide explains how to implement different privacy configurations for impact.com's Universal Tracking Tag (UTT) on a brand website and how to reflect them in the Consenter Manager.

1. Configure the third-party service.
2. Map the selected configuration in the Consenter Manager.
3. Update your privacy policy to reflect the actual implementation.

The configurations are comparative examples, not guarantees of legal compliance. Verify that the guide matches your product edition, implementation, and current provider documentation.
</Callout>

## At a glance

**Scope.** impact.com UTT and Consent Mode for brands.

**What the service does in this scope.** The UTT uses first-party cookie tracking and runs on every page. The UTT sets first-party cookies on your domain storing a UUID and timestamp to identify the customer later. Consent Mode (beta) is enabled via a support ticket; the default consent status should be declared via `impactDataLayer` before the UTT loads. Partners can append `im_rewards=1` to loyalty or rewards links. UTT version 3 can read consent directly from a standard-framework CMP; the Publisher Tag withholds `identifyUser` until consent and hides values such as `customerEmailHash`.

**Configurations included.**

| Configuration | Summary |
|---|---|
| Configuration A — Medium risk | Consent Mode on; default denied via `impactDataLayer`; Consenter updates consent; no rewards exemption. |
| Configuration B — Higher risk | As A, plus rewards/loyalty links (`im_rewards=1`) tracked without consent. |
| Configuration C — Higher risk | UTT without Consent Mode; `identify` with customer IDs/email hash. |

> ⚠ **Recommendation.** Tracking rewards traffic without consent (B) relies on a regulator position on cashback/loyalty cookies. Have your DPO assess whether it applies to your programme and jurisdiction.

## Step 1 — Configure impact.com

| # | Configuration area | Where to configure it | Configuration A — Medium risk | Configuration B — Higher risk | Configuration C — Higher risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Processing purposes | Operator decision | Support marketing analytics | Support marketing analytics; Receive marketing offers | Support marketing analytics; Receive marketing offers |
| 2 | Consent Mode | Support ticket; `impactDataLayer` | On; default denied | On; default denied | Off |
| 3 | Rewards exemption | `im_rewards=1` on partner links | Not used | Used | — |
| 4 | Customer identification | `ire('identify')` | Only after consent, no email hash | Only after consent | Customer ID and email hash |

## Step 2 — Map the configuration in the Consenter Manager

**Processing location.** impact.com is a US enterprise; list the United States.

**Legal role.** Confirm the role in your impact.com agreement; the table uses Processor for the brand-side tag.

| Consenter Manager field | Configuration A — Medium risk | Configuration B — Higher risk | Configuration C — Higher risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Processing purposes | Support marketing analytics | Support marketing analytics, Receive marketing offers | Support marketing analytics, Receive marketing offers |
| Tracking method | First party tracking (cross-session) | First party tracking (cross-session) | Third party tracking (cross-session, cross-website, cross-device) |
| Identifier | Device identifiers | Device identifiers | Device identifiers, Authentication-derived identifiers |
| Data categories | Device identifiers, Browsing and interaction data, e-commerce Activity, Privacy choices | Device identifiers, Browsing and interaction data, e-commerce Activity, Privacy choices | Device identifiers, Authentication-derived identifiers, Browsing and interaction data, e-commerce Activity |
| Legal role of data recipient | Processor | Processor | Processor |
| Personalisation model | No personalisation | No personalisation | Profile based |
| Maximum storage duration | Per programme referral window | Per programme referral window | Per programme referral window |
| Processing location | United States | United States | United States |
