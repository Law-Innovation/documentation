<Callout type="info" title="Overview">
This guide explains how to implement different privacy configurations for social sharing via ShareThis and how to reflect them in the Consenter Manager.

1. Configure the third-party service.
2. Map the selected configuration in the Consenter Manager.
3. Update your privacy policy to reflect the actual implementation.

The configurations are comparative examples, not guarantees of legal compliance. Verify that the guide matches your product edition, implementation, and current provider documentation.
</Callout>

## At a glance

**Scope.** ShareThis share and follow buttons (ShareThis is a division of Predactiv, Inc., a US data company [S1]). Sources were checked on 16 November 2026.

**What the service does in this scope.** Under the publisher terms, ShareThis may deploy cookies, pixel tags and web beacons via JavaScript when visitors view a page with ShareThis applications, and may collect, use and disclose data derived from that usage data [S2]. Personal information ShareThis collects includes cookies and IP addresses [S3]. ShareThis retains usage data for up to 14 months; its cookies expire 13 months after they are last updated [S4].

**Configurations included.**

| Configuration | Summary |
|---|---|
| Configuration A — Lower risk | Plain share links (e.g. Shariff) instead of ShareThis; nothing loaded before the click. |
| Configuration B — Higher risk | ShareThis buttons loaded after Consenter consent. |

**Configuration A without consent.** Plain links load nothing from a third party before the click; no ShareThis entry is required.

## Step 1 — Configure ShareThis

| # | Configuration area | Where to configure it | Configuration A — Lower risk | Configuration B — Higher risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Processing purposes | Operator decision | — | Unlock additional website features; Customise online ads |
| 2 | Integration | Website code | Static share links | ShareThis script [S2] |
| 3 | Loading | Consenter script blocking | — | After consent |

## Step 2 — Map the configuration in the Consenter Manager

**Processing location.** ShareThis / Predactiv is a US enterprise subject to 18 U.S.C. § 2713 [S5]; list the United States.

| Consenter Manager field | Configuration A — Lower risk | Configuration B — Higher risk |
|---|---|---|
| Entry needed | No | Yes |
| Processing purposes | — | Unlock additional website features, Customise online ads |
| Tracking method | No tracking | Third party tracking (crosssession, cross-website) |
| Identifier | No identifier | Device identifiers, IP address |
| Data categories | — | Browsing and interaction data, Device characteristics, Device identifiers, IP address, Social media interaction data, Users' profiles |
| Legal role of data recipient | — | Individual Controller |
| Personalisation model | — | Profile based |
| Maximum storage duration | — | 13 months (cookies); 14 months (usage data) [S4] |
| Processing location | — | United States |

**Basis.** ShareThis uses the usage data for its own purposes under the publisher terms [S2], hence Individual Controller.
