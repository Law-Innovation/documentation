<Callout type="info" title="Overview">
This guide explains how to implement different privacy configurations for Nosto and how to reflect them in the Consenter Manager.

1. Configure the third-party service.
2. Map the selected configuration in the Consenter Manager.
3. Update your privacy policy to reflect the actual implementation.

The configurations are comparative examples, not guarantees of legal compliance. Verify that the guide matches your product edition, implementation, and current provider documentation.
</Callout>

## At a glance

**Scope.** Nosto commerce personalisation (recommendations, search, pop-ups) via its JavaScript. Nosto Solutions Ltd is a Finnish company.

**What the service does in this scope.** `2c.cId` assigns a unique identifier to each browser for tracking visits and interactions; Nosto states that its cookies expire after four years. With `api.visit.setDoNotTrack(true)` (called before any request), Nosto runs without the `2c.cId` cookie; this does not disable cookies entirely. In the Shopware integration, Nosto without marketing consent runs in `doNotTrack` mode, sets no session identifier and sends no customer data. Nosto's Data Processing Policy and Record of Processing Activities are available in the admin.

**Configurations included.**

| Configuration | Summary |
|---|---|
| Configuration A — Medium risk | `doNotTrack` mode: non-personalised recommendations; no `2c.cId`; after consent. |
| Configuration B — Higher risk | Full tracking with `2c.cId` and customer data; after consent. |

**No configuration without prior consent.** Even in `doNotTrack` mode, cookies are not disabled entirely; Nosto advises preventing its JavaScript from loading for users who opt out of all tracking.

## Step 1 — Configure Nosto

| # | Configuration area | Where to configure it | Configuration A — Medium risk | Configuration B — Higher risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Processing purposes | Operator decision | Personalise the website (non-personalised recommendations) | Personalise the website; Receive personalised marketing offers |
| 2 | Do-not-track | `nostojs(api => api.visit.setDoNotTrack(true))` before any request | On | Off |
| 3 | Customer data | Customer tagging / integration | Not sent | Sent |
| 4 | Loading | Consenter script blocking | After consent | After consent |

## Step 2 — Map the configuration in the Consenter Manager

| Consenter Manager field | Configuration A — Medium risk | Configuration B — Higher risk |
|---|---|---|
| Processing purposes | Personalise the website | Personalise the website, Receive personalised marketing offers |
| Tracking method | First party tracking (single session) | First party tracking (cross-session) |
| Identifier | No identifier | Device identifiers, Direct identifier (if customer data is sent) |
| Data categories | Browsing and interaction data, Device characteristics | Browsing and interaction data, Device characteristics, Device identifiers, e-commerce Activity, Users' profiles, direct identifier (if customer data is sent) |
| Legal role of data recipient | Processor | Processor |
| Personalisation model | Group based (properties) | Profile based |
| Maximum storage duration | Not documented | Cookies up to 4 years |
| Processing location | Per DPA | Per DPA |
