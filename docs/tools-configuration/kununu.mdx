<Callout type="info" title="Overview">
This guide explains how to implement different privacy configurations for kununu employer widgets and how to reflect them in the Consenter Manager.

1. Configure the third-party service.
2. Map the selected configuration in the Consenter Manager.
3. Update your privacy policy to reflect the actual implementation.

The configurations are comparative examples, not guarantees of legal compliance. Verify that the guide matches your product edition, implementation, and current provider documentation.
</Callout>

## At a glance

**Scope.** kununu employer rating widgets and seals (kununu is part of New Work SE, Hamburg, Germany [S1]). Sources were checked on 16 November 2026.

**What the service does in this scope.** kununu widgets are generated on the company's kununu profile and embedded by copying the widget code [S2]. kununu does not publish which cookies or data its widgets use.

**Configurations included.**

| Configuration | Summary |
|---|---|
| Configuration A — Lower risk | Static seal image (e.g. Top Company) served from your own server with a link to the kununu profile. |
| Configuration B — Medium risk | kununu widget code loaded after Consenter consent. |

> ⚠ **Research gap.** kununu does not document the widget's device storage or data transfer. Inspect the widget in the browser before finalising the mapping for B.

## Step 1 — Configure kununu

| # | Configuration area | Where to configure it | Configuration A — Lower risk | Configuration B — Medium risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Processing purposes | Operator decision | — | Unlock additional website features |

| 3 | Loading | Consenter script blocking | — | After consent |
