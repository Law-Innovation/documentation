Typical processing purposes: Support marketing analytics, Customise online ads

> This guide explains how to reflect the Connexity merchant pixel in the Consenter Manager. Connexity documents a consent-signal mechanism but no separate configuration tiers, so this guide uses a single fixed-behaviour table.
>
> 1. Implement the service as described below.
> 2. Map the implementation in the Consenter Manager.
> 3. Update your privacy policy to reflect the actual implementation.
>
> The mapping is an example, not a guarantee of legal compliance. Verify it against your implementation and current provider documentation.

## At a glance

**Scope.** Connexity merchant pixel (conversion tracking and Dynamic Product Ads). Sources were checked on 16 November 2026.

**What the service does in this scope.** In relation to merchants, Connexity is a controller under the GDPR and, together with Taboola, uses data for affiliate and behavioural advertising [S1]. Connexity offers merchants ways to pass consent signals and to opt out of behavioural advertising [S1]. Pixel details, including the unified pixel for Dynamic Product Ads, are covered by the Merchant Privacy Terms [S2].

## Step 1 — Implement Connexity

| Configuration area | Fixed behaviour / required action |
|---|---|
| Processing purposes | Support marketing analytics; Customise online ads |
| Pixel | Load after Consenter consent and pass the consent signal [S1] |
| Behavioural advertising | Opt out via the mechanism Connexity offers if not wanted [S1] |
| Contract | Connexity Merchant Terms and Merchant Privacy Terms [S2] |

## Step 2 — Map the configuration in the Consenter Manager

| Consenter Manager field | Value to select | Basis and conditions |
|---|---|---|
| Processing purposes | Support marketing analytics, Customise online ads | [S1] |
| Tracking method | Third party tracking (crosssession, cross-website) | Behavioural advertising [S1] |
| Identifier | Device identifiers | Cookie IDs |
| Data categories | Device identifiers, Browsing and interaction data, e-commerce Activity, Users' profiles, Privacy choices | Consent signal passed [S1] |
| Legal role of data recipient | Individual Controller | [S1] |
| Personalisation model | Profile based | Behavioural advertising [S1] |
| Maximum storage duration | Per Connexity Merchant Privacy Terms | — |
| Processing location | United States | US enterprise; CLOUD Act [S3] |
