Typical processing purposes: Support marketing analytics

> This guide explains how to implement different privacy configurations for Admo.TV Analyse and how to reflect them in the Consenter Manager.
>
> 1. Configure the third-party service.
> 2. Map the selected configuration in the Consenter Manager.
> 3. Update your privacy policy to reflect the actual implementation.
>
> The configurations are comparative examples, not guarantees of legal compliance. Verify that the guide matches your product edition, implementation, and current provider documentation.

## At a glance

**Scope.** Admo.TV Analyse ("Drive to web") scripts measuring website audience generated by TV and radio advertising. Sources were checked on 16 November 2026.

**What the service does in this scope.** Admo.TV reports direct and indirect visits, conversions and visits by device type, region and country, broken down by campaign, video and investment [S1]. Three scripts are added: a consent flag `ADMO_userAdextendConsent` (called first; controls whether Admo cookies are set), a global script and conversion scripts [S1]. Cookies: `cikneeto` (session, max. 30 minutes) and `cikneeto_uuid` (13 months, user identifier) [S1]. A partial IP address (last byte truncated, immediately anonymised) is used for city-level location and bot detection [S1]. Admo.TV acts as processor [S1].

**Configurations included.**

| Configuration | Summary |
|---|---|
| Configuration A — Lower risk (⚠ no-consent) | `ADMO_userAdextendConsent = false`: no Admo cookies; traffic data only. |
| Configuration B — Higher risk | `ADMO_userAdextendConsent = true` after consent: `cikneeto` and `cikneeto_uuid` cookies. |

> ⚠ **Recommendation — exemption is self-assessed and France-specific.** CNIL treats audience-measurement tracers as exempt only under strict conditions [S2]. Describe A as a **self-assessed exemption**. § 25 TDDDG in Germany contains no equivalent exemption. Run A without consent only after review by your DPO or independent counsel.

## Step 1 — Configure Admo.TV Analyse

| # | Configuration area | Where to configure it | Configuration A — Lower risk (⚠ no-consent) | Configuration B — Higher risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Processing purposes | Operator decision | Support marketing analytics | Support marketing analytics |
| 2 | Consent flag | `ADMO_userAdextendConsent` before the global script [S1] | `false` | `true` after consent |
| 3 | Opt-out | Local opt-out in CMP/cookie page; global opt-out in Admo.TV privacy policy [S1] | Provide | Provide |

## Step 2 — Map the configuration in the Consenter Manager

| Consenter Manager field | Configuration A — Lower risk (⚠ no-consent) | Configuration B — Higher risk |
|---|---|---|
| Processing purposes | Support marketing analytics | Support marketing analytics |
| Tracking method | No tracking | First party tracking (cross-session) |
| Identifier | No identifier | Device identifiers |
| Data categories | Aggregated site statistics, Browsing and interaction data, Device characteristics, IP address anonymised, Non-precise location data | As A, plus Device identifiers, e-commerce Activity (if conversions are tracked) |
| Legal role of data recipient | Processor | Processor |
| Personalisation model | No personalisation | No personalisation |
| Maximum storage duration | Not documented | 13 months (cookie) |
| Processing location | Not documented | Not documented |

**Basis.** Admo.TV configuration guide [S1]. The behaviour in A when the flag is `false` is inferred from the flag controlling whether cookies are set; verify in the browser.
