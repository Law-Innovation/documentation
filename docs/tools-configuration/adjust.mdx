Typical processing purposes: Support marketing analytics, Customise online ads

<Callout type="info" title="Overview">
This guide explains how to implement different privacy configurations for the Adjust Web SDK and how to reflect them in the Consenter Manager.

1. Configure the third-party service.
2. Map the selected configuration in the Consenter Manager.
3. Update your privacy policy to reflect the actual implementation.

The configurations are comparative examples, not guarantees of legal compliance. Verify that the guide matches your product edition, implementation, and current provider documentation.
</Callout>

## At a glance

**Scope.** The Adjust Web SDK for web attribution and measurement. Adjust GmbH (Berlin) belongs to AppLovin Corporation, a US company.

**What the service does in this scope.** The URL strategy sets the endpoint and data residency; EU data residency uses `eu.adjust.com`. `Adjust.trackThirdPartySharing` with a `ThirdPartySharing` instance controls whether Adjust shares the user's data with third parties; granular options pass partner-specific consent (e.g. `google_dma` keys `ad_user_data`, `ad_personalization`). `gdprForgetMe` sends an erasure request.

**Configurations included.**

| Configuration | Summary |
|---|---|
| Configuration A — Medium risk | Attribution only; EU data residency; third-party sharing disabled; after consent. |
| Configuration B — Higher risk | Attribution with third-party sharing to ad partners and consent signals passed; after consent. |

**No configuration without prior consent.** Attribution requires identifying the device across visits.

## Step 1 — Configure Adjust

| # | Configuration area | Where to configure it | Configuration A — Medium risk | Configuration B — Higher risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Processing purposes | Operator decision | Support marketing analytics | Support marketing analytics; Customise online ads |
| 2 | URL strategy / data residency | `Adjust.initSdk({ urlStrategy … })` | EU data residency | EU data residency |
| 3 | Third-party sharing | `Adjust.trackThirdPartySharing(new Adjust.ThirdPartySharing(…))` | `false` | `true`, with granular partner options |
| 4 | Google DMA consent | Granular options for `google_dma` | — | Pass `ad_user_data`, `ad_personalization` from Consenter |
| 5 | Loading | Consenter script blocking | After consent | After consent |

## Step 2 — Map the configuration in the Consenter Manager

**Processing location.** EU data residency; Adjust belongs to a US group subject to the US CLOUD Act; list the United States. Each ad partner receiving data needs its own entry as an independent controller.

| Consenter Manager field | Configuration A — Medium risk | Configuration B — Higher risk |
|---|---|---|
| Processing purposes | Support marketing analytics | Support marketing analytics, Customise online ads |
| Tracking method | First party tracking (cross-session) | Third party tracking (crosssession, cross-website) |
| Identifier | Device identifiers | Device identifiers |
| Data categories | Browsing and interaction data, Device characteristics, Device identifiers, IP address | As A, plus Privacy choices, Users' profiles |
| Legal role of data recipient | Processor | Processor (Adjust); ad partners as Individual Controller |
| Personalisation model | No personalisation | Profile based |
| Maximum storage duration | Per Adjust data retention settings | Per Adjust data retention settings |
| Processing location | EU; United States | EU; United States |
