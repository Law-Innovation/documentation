Typical processing purposes: Improve the service

<Callout type="info" title="Overview">
This guide explains how to implement different privacy configurations for Jetpack Stats (WordPress plugin by Automattic) and how to reflect them in the Consenter Manager. Security features (Protect, Scan, WAF) are out of scope; sharing buttons and embeds loaded by Jetpack need their own entries.

1. Configure the third-party service.
2. Map the selected configuration in the Consenter Manager.
3. Update your privacy policy to reflect the actual implementation.

The configurations are comparative examples, not guarantees of legal compliance. Verify that the guide matches your product edition, implementation, and current provider documentation.
</Callout>

## At a glance

**Scope.** Jetpack Stats on the public front end. Sources were checked on 16 November 2026.

**What the service does in this scope.** Automattic owns and operates Jetpack; its Privacy Center links to the Automattic privacy notice and the Jetpack cookie list [S1][S2]. Automattic's cookie policy lists `tk_ni`, `tk_ai` and `tk_qs` for its first-party analytics on user sites [S3].

**Configurations included.**

| Configuration | Summary |
|---|---|
| Configuration A — No entry needed | Stats module deactivated. |
| Configuration B — Medium risk | Stats active; the Stats script is blocked until Consenter consent. |

## Step 1 — Configure Jetpack

| # | Configuration area | Where to configure it | Configuration A — No entry needed | Configuration B — Medium risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Processing purposes | Operator decision | — | Improve the service |
| 2 | Stats module | Jetpack → Settings / Modules | Off | On |
| 3 | Loading | Theme / tag management | — | After Consenter consent |

## Step 2 — Map the configuration in the Consenter Manager

**Processing location.** Automattic is a US enterprise; list the United States (18 U.S.C. § 2713 [S4]).

| Consenter Manager field | Configuration A — No entry needed | Configuration B — Medium risk |
|---|---|---|
| Entry needed | No | Yes |
| Processing purposes | — | Improve the service |
| Tracking method | No tracking | First party tracking (cross-session) |
| Identifier | — | Device identifiers |
| Data categories | — | Aggregated site statistics, Browsing and interaction data, Device identifiers, Device characteristics |
| Legal role of data recipient | — | Processor |
| Personalisation model | — | No personalisation |
| Maximum storage duration | — | Per Jetpack cookie list [S2] |
| Processing location | — | United States |
