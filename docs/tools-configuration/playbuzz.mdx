<Callout type="info" title="Overview">
This guide explains how to reflect EX.CO in the Consenter Manager. The sources do not document configurable privacy settings for EX.CO embeds, so this guide uses a single fixed-behaviour table.

1. Implement the service as described below.
2. Map the implementation in the Consenter Manager.
3. Update your privacy policy to reflect the actual implementation.

The mapping is an example, not a guarantee of legal compliance. Verify it against your implementation and current provider documentation.
</Callout>

## At a glance

**Scope.** EX.CO interactive content and EX.CO Channels video units (EX.CO Technologies Ltd., Israel).

**What the service does in this scope.** EX.CO describes its platform as generating leads and providing audience insights for targeting and segmentation; EX.CO Channels is a video unit for publishers; Evidon classifies it under ad serving and analytics/measurement. EX.CO's DPA requires each party to maintain its own privacy policy and references the ePrivacy Directive.

**No configuration without prior consent.** Ad serving and audience segmentation.

## Step 1 — Implement EX.CO

| Configuration area | Fixed behaviour / required action |
|---|---|
| Processing purposes | Unlock additional website features; Customise online ads |
| Loading | After Consenter consent |
| Contract | EX.CO DPA |
