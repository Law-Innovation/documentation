Typical processing purposes: Improve the service

> This guide explains how to implement different privacy configurations for Chartbeat and how to reflect them in the Consenter Manager.
>
> 1. Configure the third-party service.
> 2. Map the selected configuration in the Consenter Manager.
> 3. Update your privacy policy to reflect the actual implementation.
>
> The configurations are comparative examples, not guarantees of legal compliance. Verify that the guide matches your product edition, implementation, and current provider documentation.

## At a glance

**Scope.** Chartbeat content analytics script on publisher websites. Chartbeat, Inc. is a US company. Sources were checked on 16 November 2026.

**What the service does in this scope.** Chartbeat acts as a data processor; it obtains IP addresses and certain online identifiers but states it does not store IP addresses [S1][S2]. Cookies include `_cb` (site-specific visitor ID, 395 days) and `_chartbeat2` (visit timing to distinguish new, returning and loyal visitors, 395 days) [S3]. Setting `_sf_async_config.noCookies = true` prevents `_chartbeat2` from being created [S4]. Visitors can opt out via Chartbeat's opt-out page (`_cb_optout`) [S3].

**Configurations included.**

| Configuration | Summary |
|---|---|
| Configuration A — Medium risk | `noCookies` enabled; after consent. |
| Configuration B — Higher risk (default) | Cookies `_cb` and `_chartbeat2` (395 days); after consent. |

**No configuration without prior consent.** Chartbeat runs a script that sends frequent pings with engagement data; no consent-free configuration is documented.

## Step 1 — Configure Chartbeat

| # | Configuration area | Where to configure it | Configuration A — Medium risk | Configuration B — Higher risk (default) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Processing purposes | Operator decision | Improve the service | Improve the service |
| 2 | Cookies | `_sf_async_config.noCookies` [S4] | `true` | `false` (default) |
| 3 | Loading | Consenter script blocking | After consent | After consent |
| 4 | Opt-out | Link to Chartbeat opt-out page [S3] | Provide | Provide |

## Step 2 — Map the configuration in the Consenter Manager

**Processing location.** Chartbeat, Inc. is a US company subject to 18 U.S.C. § 2713 [S5].

| Consenter Manager field | Configuration A — Medium risk | Configuration B — Higher risk (default) |
|---|---|---|
| Processing purposes | Improve the service | Improve the service |
| Tracking method | First party tracking (single session) | First party tracking (cross-session) |
| Identifier | IP address | Device identifiers, IP address |
| Data categories | Aggregated site statistics, Browsing and interaction data, Device characteristics, IP address | Aggregated site statistics, Browsing and interaction data, Device characteristics, Device identifiers, IP address |
| Legal role of data recipient | Processor | Processor |
| Personalisation model | No personalisation | No personalisation |
| Maximum storage duration | Not documented | 395 days (cookies) [S3] |
| Processing location | United States | United States |

**Basis.** Processor role and IP handling [S1][S2]; cookies [S3]; `noCookies` [S4]. In A, verify in the browser whether `_cb` is also suppressed; if not, use the B mapping for identifier and tracking method.
