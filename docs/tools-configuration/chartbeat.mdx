<Callout type="info" title="Overview">
This guide explains how to implement different privacy configurations for Chartbeat and how to reflect them in the Consenter Manager.

1. Configure the third-party service.
2. Map the selected configuration in the Consenter Manager.
3. Update your privacy policy to reflect the actual implementation.

The configurations are comparative examples, not guarantees of legal compliance. Verify that the guide matches your product edition, implementation, and current provider documentation.
</Callout>

## At a glance

**Scope.** Chartbeat content analytics script on publisher websites. Chartbeat, Inc. is a US company.

**What the service does in this scope.** Chartbeat acts as a data processor; it obtains IP addresses and certain online identifiers but states it does not store IP addresses. Cookies include `_cb` (site-specific visitor ID, 395 days) and `_chartbeat2` (visit timing to distinguish new, returning and loyal visitors, 395 days). Setting `_sf_async_config.noCookies = true` prevents `_chartbeat2` from being created. Visitors can opt out via Chartbeat's opt-out page (`_cb_optout`).

**Configurations included.**

| Configuration | Summary |
|---|---|
| Configuration A — Medium risk | `noCookies` enabled; after consent. |
| Configuration B — Higher risk (default) | Cookies `_cb` and `_chartbeat2` (395 days); after consent. |

**No configuration without prior consent.** Chartbeat runs a script that sends frequent pings with engagement data; no consent-free configuration is documented.

## Step 1 — Configure Chartbeat

| # | Configuration area | Where to configure it | Configuration A — Medium risk | Configuration B — Higher risk (default) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Processing purposes | Operator decision | Improve the service | Improve the service |
| 2 | Cookies | `_sf_async_config.noCookies` | `true` | `false` (default) |
| 3 | Loading | Consenter script blocking | After consent | After consent |
| 4 | Opt-out | Link to Chartbeat opt-out page | Provide | Provide |
