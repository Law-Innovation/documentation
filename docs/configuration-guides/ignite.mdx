How to embed [Ignite](https://ignite.video/en/articles/basics) on your website and properly map these configurations within the customer panel to ensure users are clearly informed about the use of this Third Party Provider (TPP). The tables below outline how embedding Ignite on your website affects the privacy risks for users.

<Callout type="info">
  **Low Privacy Risk:** Ignite is designed as a privacy-friendly video embedding
  solution that minimizes data collection and third-party connections.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
  **No Tracking:** Unlike YouTube, Instagram, or Twitter embeds, Ignite does not
  track users or create connections to advertising networks or social media
  platforms.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
  **Consent Considerations:** In many jurisdictions, Ignite's low-risk
  configuration may not require explicit consent due to minimal data processing.
  However, always consult local privacy laws.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
  **EU Hosting Option:** Ignite can be configured to process and store all data
  within the EU, eliminating concerns about US CLOUD Act or international data
  transfers.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
  **Data Processor Role:** Ignite acts as a Data Processor under your control,
  not as an independent Controller like YouTube or social media platforms.
</Callout>

## Privacy relevant configurations (Parameters)

This table shows features which are enabled or disabled within **Ignite**. Use this overview to ensure your Ignite setup and Consenter Manager settings stay consistent.

Each row represents a feature that can be disabled or enabled in various ways when configuring your TPP for your website. The left-hand column describes the feature, while the right-hand column provides guidance on where to find it in the **Consenter Manager** and how to configure it to accurately reflect your TPP setup.

<Callout type="info">
  Some functions or data categories may be named differently between TPPs or in
  our overview due to the lack of standardization. **As the data controller, you
  are responsible for informing users in a clear and comprehensible manner**.
  This guide supports you by offering uniform, established terminology that
  helps users understand how their personal data is processed, thereby fostering
  trust in your brand.
</Callout>

| **Parameters**                | **➡️ Ignite Config Low Risk (No Consent)** | **➡️ Consenter Manager Config** What to indicate in Consenter Manager when embedding Ignite videos on your website                                 |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Consent**                   | No                                         | _You may select Ignite as the data recipient to inform users about the service. However, prior consent blocking of Ignite videos is not required._ |
| **Data processing agreement** | Yes - Ignite acts as Data Processor        | Select: Data Processor as legal role of data recipient                                                                                             |
| **Tracking method**           | No tracking                                | Select respective tracking method.                                                                                                                 |
| **Identifier**                | No persistent identifiers                  | -                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Retention Period**          | Delete after session                       | Select: Purpose fulfillment                                                                                                                        |
| **Processing location**       | EU only (if configured)                    | Select EU as data processing location. Ignite can be configured for EU-only hosting.                                                               |
| **Advertising Features**      | No                                         | No additional marketing purpose required                                                                                                           |
