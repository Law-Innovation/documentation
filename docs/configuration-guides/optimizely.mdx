How to configure [Optimizely](https://www.optimizely.com/de/products/personalization/) and properly map these configurations within the customer panel to ensure users are clearly informed about the use of this Third Party Provider (TPP). The tables below outline how different configurations of Optimizely affect the privacy risks for users.

<Callout type="warn">
  **Privacy Risk Level:** Optimizely's privacy risk varies significantly based
  on configuration.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warn">
  **Consent Requirement:** Always obtain explicit opt-in consent before loading
  Optimizely.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warn">
  **CRM Integration Risk:** Integrating Optimizely with CRM systems (Salesforce,
  HubSpot) significantly increases privacy risk by linking anonymous behavioral
  data to identified users.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warn">
  **Cross-Platform Tracking:** High-risk configurations enable tracking users
  across multiple properties and devices through user ID matching.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warn">
  **EU vs US Hosting:** Optimizely offers EU data residency options, but the
  company is US-based and subject to US data access laws.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warn">
  **Third-Party Integrations:** Each integration (Google Analytics, Facebook
  Pixel, etc.) adds additional privacy considerations and may require separate
  consent.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warn">
  **Data Minimization:** Configure Optimizely to collect only the minimum data
  necessary for your specific use cases.
</Callout>
