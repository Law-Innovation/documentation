<summary>Begründung &amp; Quellen</summary>

**Key Facts:**
Yandex is a Russian technology company. Any data transferred to or accessed from Russia is subject to Russian surveillance legislation (Federal Law No. 374-FZ and related laws), which provide Russian security services broad access to telecommunications and internet data without meaningful judicial oversight — a clear RF2. Yandex also operates its own advertising ecosystem with cross-service profiling (RF1). European operations reduced following 2022 geopolitical changes but residual Russia exposure remains.

**Framework Note (v2.2):**
FRAMEWORK NOTE (v2.2): RF2 (high-risk third-country exposure) is recorded here as a significant risk factor and lowers the domain E score. Under the revised framework, RF2 alone does not trigger Caution — the Caution badge for this service is driven by RF1 / RF3 / RF4 (as noted above). The US transfer risk (where applicable) is covered by DPF participation and/or SCCs, which GDPR treats as a compliant transfer mechanism.
