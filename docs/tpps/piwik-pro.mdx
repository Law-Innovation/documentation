**Key Facts:**
Piwik PRO is a Polish privacy-focused analytics platform with strong GDPR orientation. EU data hosting is available and the default for most European clients. DPA available; no advertising profiling; sub-processors disclosed. However, Piwik PRO acknowledges some use of aggregate anonymised data for product improvement. US transfer is possible in certain configurations. The platform comes close to Privacy+ but lacks the public CNIL or equivalent certification that would confirm all criteria without reservation. A strong No badge — the most privacy-friendly outcome in this category.
