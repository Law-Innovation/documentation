**Key Facts:**
ShareThis social sharing widgets collect cross-site user data (pages visited, content interacted with) which ShareThis uses to build and sell audience segments to advertisers — a textbook own-purpose cross-site profiling operation (RF1). US company; US transfer (RF2). ShareThis has faced FTC scrutiny.

**Framework Note (v2.2):**
FRAMEWORK NOTE (v2.2): RF2 (high-risk third-country exposure) is recorded here as a significant risk factor and lowers the domain E score. The US transfer risk (where applicable) is covered by DPF participation and/or SCCs, which GDPR treats as a compliant transfer mechanism.
