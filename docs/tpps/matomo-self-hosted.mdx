<summary>Reasoning &amp; Sources</summary>

**Key Facts:**
In on-premise deployment, all analytics data resides exclusively on the controller's own servers. InnoCraft Ltd. (developer) processes no visitor data. The controller acts as sole controller and processor simultaneously — no third-party data sharing occurs. In its cookieless configuration, the French CNIL confirmed Matomo qualifies for the audience measurement consent exemption. The ePrivacy Seal independently certified Matomo's consent-free operation. Open-source codebase allows full auditability. Robust deletion and data-subject-rights tools are built in. No international transfer risk for on-premise deployment.
