<details>
<summary>Begründung &amp; Quellen</summary>

**Key Facts:**
Twitter/X is an advertising-funded platform with extensive user profiling for targeted advertising (RF1). X Internet Unlimited Company (Dublin) is EEA controller but all data processing and access occur in the US (RF2). FTC fined Twitter $150 million (May 2022) for using 2FA phone numbers to serve targeted ads — documented purpose violation. NOYB filed complaints in 9 EU countries (Aug 2024) over Twitter/X training Grok AI on EU user data without consent; Irish DPC obtained emergency court order halting this (RF4 — opacity regarding AI data use). Irish DPC previously fined Twitter €450,000 (Dec 2020) for data breach notification failures.

**Framework Note (v2.2):**
FRAMEWORK NOTE (v2.2): RF2 (high-risk third-country exposure) is recorded here as a significant risk factor and lowers the domain E score. Under the revised framework, RF2 alone does not trigger Caution — the Caution badge for this service is driven by RF1 / RF3 / RF4 (as noted above). The US transfer risk (where applicable) is covered by DPF participation and/or SCCs, which GDPR treats as a compliant transfer mechanism.
