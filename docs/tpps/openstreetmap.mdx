<details>
<summary>Reasoning &amp; Sources</summary>

**Key Facts:**
OpenStreetMap is operated by the OpenStreetMap Foundation (OSMF), a UK nonprofit. No advertising model exists and no commercial profiling occurs — strongly positive. However, OSMF is an independent controller for tile service users — a DPA relationship between the embedding website and OSMF is legally unavailable. OSMF acknowledged in its 2024 State of the Map conference that its GDPR compliance 'has lagged behind requirements in some respects' and is under remediation. Sub-processor and CDN chain not fully publicly documented. UK post-Brexit adequacy decision applies to UK-based processing; some infrastructure nodes are outside EEA.

**Quellen:**
- OSMF Privacy Policy (osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy)
- OSMF Tiles Usage Policy (operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/tiles)
- OSMF GDPR wiki page
- SotM 2024 GDPR/compliance talk (State of the Map 2024).
