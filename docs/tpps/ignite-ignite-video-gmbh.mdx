<summary>Reasoning &amp; Sources</summary>

**Key Facts:**
Ignite Video GmbH (Düsseldorf, Germany) is a video hosting platform built from the ground up to operate without any cookies and without requiring visitor consent. The system does not process any personal data from website visitors: IP addresses are deleted immediately on connection, no tracking scripts are deployed, and all analytics are fully anonymised (aggregate only). All servers are located in Germany. Law firm Heidrich Rechtsanwälte (Hanover) issued a written legal opinion confirming that no consent requirement, cookie notice, or data protection documentation obligation applies to the embedded player. DPA available for B2B clients; sub-processors are EU-based and contractually bound.

**Quellen:**
