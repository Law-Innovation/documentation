<summary>Reasoning &amp; Sources</summary>

**Key Facts:**
Eulerian Technologies (Paris, France) is one of the most established tools on CNIL's consent-exemption list, used by major French government portals including service-public.gouv.fr (the French government services portal) and impots.gouv.fr (the French tax authority) — the highest-trust public-sector reference available. The server-side first-party data collection architecture means no direct contact between user terminals and Eulerian servers in standard deployment, an exceptionally strong privacy-enhancing design. Processes data exclusively on behalf of the controller; no own-purpose advertising; no cross-site tracking; EU/France-only hosting. DPA contractual. Clients include Carrefour, Fnac Darty, Orange, Canal+.
