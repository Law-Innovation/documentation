<details>
<summary>Begründung &amp; Quellen</summary>

**Key Facts:**
AWIN is a pure-play affiliate tracking network. In its standard operation, AWIN tracks referral clicks, publisher IDs, and conversion events — no own-purpose behavioural advertising profiling. A DPA is automatically incorporated in the advertiser and publisher agreements. AWIN processes on a legitimate-interest legal basis (LI Assessment available). However, AWIN acts as a joint controller with advertisers and publishers (not a pure processor), reducing controller oversight. Global network with US operations; DPF and SCCs. Cookie consent may be required for AWIN tracking cookies under national ePrivacy rules.
