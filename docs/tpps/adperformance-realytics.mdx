<details>
<summary>Reasoning &amp; Sources</summary>

**Key Facts:**
AdPerformance (Realytics SAS, Paris) is one of the very few TV attribution tools to obtain CNIL consent exemption — confirmed in Realytics' own published announcement: 'AdPerformance takes a major step forward in the protection of consumer privacy with official exemption from the CNIL.' Configured without persistent individual-level identifiers; no cross-site profiling; anonymous audience measurement of TV ad impact on website visits only. Realytics processes data as processor for the advertiser-controller; no own-purpose profiling. France-based; EU hosting. DPA available. Clients include TF1, M6, France Télévisions, Canal+.

**Quellen:**
- Realytics 2023 highlights blog post (blog.realytics.io)
