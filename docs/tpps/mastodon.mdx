<details>
<summary>Reasoning &amp; Sources</summary>

**Key Facts:**
Mastodon is a decentralised open-source social platform with no commercial advertising model, no cross-service profiling, and no own-purpose commercial data use — strongly privacy-positive characteristics. For mastodon.social (operated by Mastodon gGmbH, Berlin, Germany), the posture is clearly Privacy+: EU hosting, German nonprofit controller, no advertising. However, a single badge cannot be responsibly assigned to 'Mastodon' as a category because: (a) the controller varies per instance; (b) data location may be non-EU; (c) no DPA can be concluded with most instance operators; (d) retention and security practices vary. Controllers embedding Mastodon content should assess the specific instance used.

**Quellen:**
- Mastodon gGmbH Privacy Policy (mastodon.social/privacy-policy)
- Mastodon general privacy documentation (docs.joinmastodon.org)
- Mastodon gGmbH registration (Amtsgericht Berlin-Charlottenburg).
