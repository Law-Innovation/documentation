<summary>Begründung &amp; Quellen</summary>

**Key Facts:**
Vimeo's Privacy Policy explicitly states 'we use your data to show you ads on third-party sites, and to measure the effectiveness of those ads' — a direct own-advertising-use statement (approaching RF1 for standard user accounts). DPA is available only for Enterprise/OTT tier customers; for standard embed use, Vimeo acts as an independent controller, not a processor, meaning a full DPA arrangement is unavailable to regular controllers. Data primarily processed in the US; DPF participation. Privacy Mode and DNT embed parameter reduce tracking in configured deployments but are not default.
