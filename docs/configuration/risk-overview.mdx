---
title: Risk Overview Page
description: Analyse the risks and benefits of your configuration, compare changes over time, and manage risk-related user notifications.
---

On this page, you are presented with a summary of the risks and benefits associated with each configured purpose. Your setup is compared both to the risk level of an average website and—if applicable—to the risks of your previous configuration.

If you are modifying an existing cookie banner, this page also functions as a control center for the trigger system. This system allows you to notify returning visitors about increases in risk or to request consent again from users who previously refused, if you have reduced the data protection risks for them (see section [Changes in the Risk–Benefit Ratio](../getting-started/risk-assessment#changes-in-the-riskbenefit-ratio-trigger-system)).
