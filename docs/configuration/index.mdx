---
title: Configure Your Banner
description: Get started with configuring your Consenter Cookie Banner.
---

Use the Consenter Manager to configure your cookie banner.

Before you begin:

1. Create a Consenter Manager account and choose the subscription that best fits your needs. If you're unsure which subscription is right for you, start with the free trial.
2. Create a new site and enter your website's domain.
3. Follow the configuration steps in this section to configure your banner.
4. Use our provider guides whenever you configure third-party tools.
