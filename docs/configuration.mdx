---
title: Configure Your Banner
description: Get started with configuring your Consenter Cookie Banner.
---

Use the [Consenter Manager](https://manager.consenter.eu/) to configure your cookie banner.

Before you begin:

1. Create a Consenter Manager account and choose the subscription that best fits your needs. If you're unsure which subscription is right for you, start with the free trial.
2. Create a new site and enter your website's domain.
3. Identify the technologies on your website that collect your users' personal data (see [Identify Your Technologies](/identify-technologies)).
4. Follow the configuration steps in this section to configure your banner.
5. Use our [risk configuration guides](/tools-configuration) whenever you configure third-party tools.
